# Taste

Run this before you hand a draft or a design to the owner. A miss you can fix without inventing a fact, fix it and check again. A miss that needs the owner, stop and name it. If `brand.local/taste.md` exists, run those lines too.

Do not tell the owner it passed unless you did the checks.

## Copy

- The piece does the job the brief named, and no second job.
- Identity and voice match `brand.local/identity.md` and `brand.local/voice.md`.
- Banned words from voice are absent.
- The first lines carry the point. A LinkedIn hook is a complete promise inside about 210 characters.
- The close is a real next step, or the owner asked for none.
- Each factual sentence has a source you can open. Proof sources are the ones identity lists.
- No number, customer, or result was added to make a line land.
- Lines on a picture are instructions or facts already in the post. Verb first when the line is an instruction. At most three lines under a plate name.

## Design

- Tokens come from `brand.local/tokens.css`. Preview stand-ins were not handed over as the brand.
- Type sizes are the ones in style. Text was not shrunk to fit. A tight frame became another slide.
- Reading order is stated, and the layout follows it.
- Plates share one nameplate style. Connectors do not cross and do not cover type.
- Text is real HTML, not outlines.
- Logo, photo, or icon appears only if `brand.local/assets/README.md` allows that use.
- No generated, retouched, or replaced face or body.
- The export matches the size in style, or the repo target you named because style left that row blank.
- You looked at 1x and at phone width.

## Motion

- Motion is the JavaScript timeline in the same HTML file (`DeskTimeline`, `window.__seek`). CSS did not animate it.
- The still is that timeline's last frame, from the same source.
- Parts arrive in reading order, 0.4s each, ease-out, no bounce.
- The call to action is fully on screen by 2.3s.
- The hold starts and ends on the same pixels.
- The GIF's first frame is the empty stage.
- A still of the last frame exists even if the motion file failed.
- You checked a filmstrip, not only the script's exit code.

## Accessibility

- Text contrasts with its surface. If you did not check, say so. Do not claim a pass you did not look at.
- The point is in the text, not only in color.
- Type is large enough to read at the export size and at phone width.
- Motion is not required to understand the still.
- Captions, when the piece is video, follow the words that were spoken and do not cover the mouth.

## Numbers

- Every figure traces to a tool response, a Company Context page, or a file the owner supplied.
- Unknown is not written as zero.
- An example in this repo is labeled as an example and is not presented as the owner's result.
