# AI tells

Every drafted post, quote, reply, comment, DM, and plan line passes this list before the owner sees it. It sits on top of the stock banned words in `scripts/queue-rules.json` and the `## Banned words` section of `brand.local/voice.md`. It does not replace either list.

A tell is a habit that makes copy read as machine written. Cut it, then reread the line out loud.

| Tell | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Long dash | The long dash between two clauses | A period, a comma, or and |
| Stock opener | "Here's the thing", "Let's dive in", "In today's world", "Buckle up" | Start with the claim or the scene |
| Flip frame | "It's not X, it's Y" and its cousins ("This isn't about X. It's about Y.") | Say Y and give the reason |
| Tidy triplet | Three parallel items that land on a beat, used by reflex | Keep the one item that carries the point, or the real count |
| Stacked adjectives | Two or more adjectives in front of one noun ("a bold, simple, powerful idea") | One concrete word, or a number |
| Hype listicle | "7 tools that will change everything", a list of claims with no receipts | One sourced point the owner can stand behind |
| Hashtag wall | Three or more hashtags, or hashtags inside the sentence | None, or one the owner's own posts use |
| Closing bait | A question at the end that asks for engagement ("Thoughts?", "Agree?") | End on the point. Keep a closing question only when the owner's own posts end that way |
| Empty intensifier | "truly", "incredibly", "game-changing" in place of a fact | The fact |
| Fake intimacy | "Let me be honest", "I'll be real with you" | Drop it |

Check the owner's own habits in `brand.local/voice.md` before you cut a pattern they really use. A habit their own posts repeat, cited by example, wins over this list, except the long dash, which never stays.
