# Skill frame

Every skill under `skills/` follows this file. The skill itself is one line plus the procedure. Do not paste this page back into the skill.

## Read

Before a draft or a design, read `brand/AGENTS.md`, then the filled files in `brand.local/`. `Status: blank`, or a missing file, means stop and ask. Do not guess a color, a font, a voice line, or a claim.

Before you write for a reader, score someone, or qualify them, read `audience/AGENTS.md`, then `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`. The reader is that file. The buyer is the Ideal Customer page in Company Context. Do not merge them. Do not treat the template questions as the owner's answers.

A required unknown on the qualifying test is a hold, not a no and not a yes. A required no with evidence on the row fails the person and stops. A no without evidence is unsure, so it holds. Engagement does not answer the test. A company fail stops even when the person test is yes. Hold is not enrolled and is not sent.

## Hand off

Run `brand/taste.md` before the owner sees the work. The preview stand-in in `pictures/timeline.js` is not the brand. Do not hand that render over as finished.

## Consent

Nothing posts, comments, sends a DM, enrolls, launches, spends, schedules, or deletes without the owner's yes on that exact item. Every tool is fetched with `tools_get` before `tools_run`. The fetched schema wins over this repo. When that mode exists, dry_run first, then live with `quote_hash`, `max_credits`, and `idempotency_key`. A refused quote is a price, not a yes. Some reads refuse dry_run and run live if you send that mode. The skill names those.

## Pictures

Graphics are HTML and CSS. Motion is a JavaScript `DeskTimeline` that drives HTML, CSS, SVG, and canvas. Capture calls `__seek`. The still is the last frame of that same timeline. CSS does not animate.

## Facts

Do not invent a metric, a face, or a connection. Dreamstate plus local tools only. Ids and arguments are in `docs/tool-map.md`.

## Writing

Drafted copy uses no em dash. That covers posts, comments, replies, DMs, newsletters, scripts, titles, descriptions, and articles. Use a period, a comma, or and. Comments and replies also use no en dash.

Stock banned words are the `banned` lists in `scripts/queue-rules.json`. Words under `## Banned words` in `brand.local/voice.md` add to the list for that run. They do not replace it. A writing skill does not copy either list. Its stop rule is: Follow the skill-frame writing rules.
