# What stays

Leave the title, the argument, the section sequence, the examples, the links, the figures, and the voice as they stand.

The one link edit is on the owner's own site: set `utm_source=linkedin` and keep the weekly campaign slug already on the essay and the brief, so these readers are not filed under the essay's other channel.

# What may change

Change a sentence only when LinkedIn's article editor would make the essay's version worse: remove a reference that exists only for the other channel, retune an opening or a close that assumes that channel, and repair formatting that is actually broken.

If the essay is already about LinkedIn, change little.

Numbers, windows of time, qualifiers, and the split between what was shown and what is only proposed stay at the same scope.

Check them on the Proof page.
