# Share post

When the handoff needs a description or a post for the network, write those as two pieces, in the voice in `brand.local/voice.md`. Keep the description short. The post can be pasted as it stands.

Open that post on the result, using the owner's words. A number appears only when the Proof page has cleared it, and the window of time for that number stays in the same sentence.

The middle is the earlier state, the later state, and how the change works. Do not turn that middle into tips.

Tell the reader why opening the article is worth it. Leave the article's procedure inside the article. Public figures stay inside the Proof page.

The result is what shows before the fold. The last line is an ask or a question.

The post points at the article. It tells the reader the link sits in the comment under the post.

That comment is the article URL with `utm_source=linkedin` and the same weekly campaign. Do not lift a sample post, its story, or its stand-in counts.
