# Capture

Run a low-cost preview before the full encode. Seek the start, one middle beat, and the last frame. Check pace, bounds, labels, and continuity for the objects and for the camera.

Then capture the whole timeline. The still is the last frame.

Hold the last frame so a loop does not blink the call to action off: when the piece lasts at least 2.4 seconds, the pixels at 2.4 seconds match the last frame, and the call to action is still on.
