# Stage

Fewer, bigger stations. Keep every actor in view. The same nameplate style on every actor: a number, a name, and at most three lines.

A line states an instruction, or a fact the post already contains. Do not shrink the type. Put the name down before the smaller tags.

Cut a prop that hides a label. Words are real HTML, not a picture of text. Labels do not cover a station or each other. The frame stays fixed.
