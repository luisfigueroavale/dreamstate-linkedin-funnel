# Evidence

Every claim names a measurement they supplied: the url, the views, the channel median, the ratio, and the capture date.

A fetched page may date a launch. It does not supply a view count.

One viral video is an anecdote.

Call a trend or a title shape validated only after 3 or more outliers, each at 3× or above, across 3 or more channels and 2 or more niches.

Log each verdict in `vault/notes/packaging-library.md`.

A tip is counted the same day with this sheet before it is evidence.

Their own uploads are proven only in the ledger.

A ratio is current views against that channel's comparable uploads. It is not click-through, not views per hour, and not causation.

Never call the age-band ratio velocity.

Files and numbers come from the owner.
