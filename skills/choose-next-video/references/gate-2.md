# Gate 2

The niche picks the topic. Packaging is proved elsewhere. When the same shape wins across 2 or more unrelated niches, the shape is what won. Inside one niche the idea, the title, and the still are tangled.

## 1. Titles

Draft 3 to 5 titles. Each names the wave and the niche outcome. `write-video-titles` may polish the pair. It does not replace this donor test.

## 2. One structure

One structure at a time, from 4 to 6 niches, at least 2 outside their field. Own seed file. `branch` is the donating niche. Window 90 days. Depth 0. `keep` is the structure. `fit` is the niche outcome. Do not merge them.

## 3. Three-part pass

Apply the three-part pass below.

## 4. Stills

Open the top 5 donor stills they paste. Record subject (to-camera, back-shot, object, or UI), the one hero object, word count, and contrast.

Brief `study-thumbnail-patterns` and `design-video-thumbnail`. On-image words name the niche outcome. The hero is the wave's mark. About 2 to 4 words.

No other creator's face unless the video is about that person.

## 5. Promise check

Promise check. The title and the still claim only what the video shows. A number only when Proof has cleared it.

Hand over 2 titles and 2 still directions, each with donors (url, niche, ratio) and the saturation check. The owner picks.

Donor fields and the eight structures (experiment, equation, time box, removed task, verdict, versus, plea, swap), with the `packaging` name and the shape the scorer tests, are in `skills/choose-next-video/references/score-sheet.md`.

## Donors file

`donors.json` uses `rows` for outside niches and `niche_rows` for the wear test. Optional `structure` drops rows whose title misses that shape.

A row with no ratio or no age is ignored, not treated as zero. Window fields are `window_days` 90 and `niche_window_days` 30.

## Pass, all three

Proved elsewhere means, inside 90 days, at least 3 outliers at 3× or above, at least 3 channels, and at least 2 donor niches.

Not worn at home means the same shape on 3 or 4 niche seeds across 30 days.

Worn means 3 or more home-niche channels used it and the median of their ratios sits under 1.5×. That median averages the two central ratios on an even count. A median of 1.5× is not under the line. A missing ratio stays missing.

Transfer the skeleton. Preserve word order, any parenthesis, and the slot where a number sits. Put the wave and the niche outcome into those slots, then run the promise check.

## Library

Read the packaging library before you draft.

After each check, record the shape, when you counted it, each donor's url, niche, and ratio, whether the home niche is saturated, and a verdict: validated, worn, or not proven.

Below 10 videos of their own, a pattern in their old stills is a hint, not a rule. After the ledger has rows, read those stills for the subject, the object, and the words on hits and missing on misses.
