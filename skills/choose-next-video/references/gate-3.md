# Gate 3

Gate 3 is the first minute. The owner reads it aloud.

Script minute one as 5 beats. Aim near 160 words. A clear take may stretch toward 220. The template band is 150 to 170. Every beat names the picture.

Sentence one restates the title. Frame one is the hero object from the still. Write three alternates of sentence one. The owner reads them aloud, picks, and films that opening before the rest.

`edit-long-video` uses the cut marks.

A studio paste uses the share still watching at 0:30. High means the opening delivered the package. Low means change the title, the still, or the opening.

There is no universal benchmark. Compare with this channel's previous 10 of similar length when they paste that. Prove the promise, then show the work.

On a business channel, use at most 3 short lines, restate the promise, raise curiosity past the packaging, and enter point 1.

From transcripts they supply of their last 3 or 4 videos, note the first 15 seconds, 0:15 through 1:00, and keep or cut. Count "essentially", "actually", "basically", and the words in minute one. That is their pace.

Outlier openings often land around 200 to 270 words and open on the proof object, a numbered promise against a contrast, the belief this video breaks, or the wave with its date. A disclaimer or another creator is a rare open.

## Beats

1. Confirm, 0:00 through 0:05, cap 15 words. State the title's promise as an outcome. Do not greet. Do not say their name. The picture is the hero object, and it is live.

2. Stakes, 0:05 through 0:15, cap 30 words. Say why this week. Show a launch page, an outlier, or the mark.

3. Receipt, 0:15 through 0:25, cap 25 words. One line of cleared proof, plus half a line on who they are. Show that number on their display.

4. Loop, from 25 seconds through 45 seconds, cap 50 words. Name the three parts, and leave one loop open on the part that produced the result. Show the card that lists those parts, or show the map.

5. Payoff, from 45 seconds through one minute, cap 45 words. Begin part 1. Do not use a hop-in line. Show step 1.

Caps add to 165. Do not pad. Show the result, then the method. Benchmarks, history, and theory wait until after the first payoff.

No other creators, filler, subscribe ask, channel intro, or sponsor in this minute. Change the picture every 3 to 5 seconds for the first 30 seconds.

Near 3:00, open another loop or show a result they have not seen.

## Handed script

The handed script has TITLE, HERO, then the five beats with three confirm lines labeled A, B, and C, and a WORDS line.

`opening` looks for those five time marks, three confirm lines, a beat over its cap, any confirm line over 15 words, a spoken total over 220, a hop-in on the payoff, and a confirm that starts with hi, hey, or hello.

The spoken total takes the line marked `PICK` when present, otherwise the first confirm line, then beats 2 through 5. Outside 150 to 170 is a note unless the total exceeds 220.

Filler and a missing cut mark inside the first three beats are notes. A note is not a pass on the promise.

Once the video is up, and only after they paste the figures, write the 0:30 share and the 1:00 share into the ledger. If the script beats the previous 10 at 0:30, keep beat 1. Otherwise write a new beat 1.
