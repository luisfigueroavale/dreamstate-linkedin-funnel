# Ledger

The ledger is the day after, three days, a week, and four weeks (28 days). Record studio click-through and retention only.

After publish, record in `vault/notes/channel-ledger.md` at the day after, at three days, at a week, and at four weeks: views, views per day against the channel median they supplied, and, only from a studio paste, impressions, click-through, the 0:30 still-watching share, the 1:00 still-watching share, and average view duration.

Leave a studio cell blank when they have not pasted it. Do not write zero. Add this video's card and one sentence on the lesson.

When a card of 8 or more misses, or a card under 8 hits, revise the rubric and write the reason.

The week-out pace is the calibration column. Views per day are views divided by age in days, age at least 1. Compare pace with pace. Do not divide unlike units. Do not use the token sequence "d 1 d 3 d 7".

Until the ledger has 3 or more rows, the rubric is uncalibrated. Below 3 ledger rows, the sheet is uncalibrated.
