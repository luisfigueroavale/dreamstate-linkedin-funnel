# Scorecard

Five criteria, each 0, 1, or 2. Pass is 8 or higher, and Heat, Gap, and Fit must each be above 0.

Backtest the card on their uploads, and revise it when the ledger gains a row. `card` sums the five integers. If Heat is 0 while Gap is above 0, it warns: a dead wave does not create a gap.

## Heat

Heat is whether the wave is hot now. Evidence is the heat table and the top outliers.

2: at least 3 outliers at 3× or higher, from at least 2 channels, freshest younger than 14 days. 1: outliers are 15 to 30 days old, or only one channel produced them. 0: no wave, evergreen, or outliers absent.

## Gap

Gap is whether the niche angle is still open. Evidence is the probes and the list in step 4.

2: niche uploads on this wave lack outlier numbers, and there are not many of them. 1: plenty of niche uploads, small or near baseline, and none is an outlier. 0: a larger channel in this niche already has an outlier on the angle.

Give Gap points only when Heat is 1 or 2.

## Fit

Fit is whether the outcome is this owner's niche. Evidence is the title draft and the follower file.

2: the title states the outcome this audience pays for. 1: adjacent work they care about less. 0: a different audience, personal growth, or general news.

Heat is measured off their channel. Fit is measured on it. A personal-brand idea passes only when the outcome is the niche outcome.

## Timing

Timing is whether it can ship while the window is open. 2: the upload goes up within 14 days of the wave's beginning, or of its latest jump. 1: the lag is 15 to 30 days. 0: later than that.

On one wave, Gap and Timing usually split a hit from a miss.

## Receipt

Receipt is whether the owner can show the thing. Evidence is Proof, already cleared.

2: a real run, on screen, with a cleared number, and that number is required before anyone records. 1: a real run, and no number. 0: opinion only, or an explainer with nothing they ran.

Receipt at 0 does not alone fail a card whose total is still 8 or more while Heat, Gap, and Fit stay above 0. An idea they cannot run is still Receipt 0.

## Two waves

One idea may carry two waves when both serve the niche outcome. If the idea repeats 1 of their last 3 videos, note it on the slate. A follow-up on a winner may stay. A rerun may not.
