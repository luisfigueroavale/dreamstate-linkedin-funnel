# Treat the audio

Measure with ffmpeg `volumedetect` or `loudnorm` in print mode. Change gain if the voice is clearly quiet or hot. A high-pass only if rumble is audible. Keep the untreated file. Listen is the owner's. You report the numbers the filter printed. You do not claim it sounds better without those numbers.

## Done

The measurements before and after, and the filter you applied.
