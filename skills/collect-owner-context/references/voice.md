# Voice

Accepted samples go in `vault/voice/`, and they stay uncommitted.

Rules they stand behind (person, sentence length, refused words, close) go on Tone of Voice.

A five-pair card goes to `derive-voice-rules`.

For type, color, and pictures, write only supplied values into `brand.local/style.md`, `brand.local/tokens.css`, or `brand.local/assets/`.

Do not draw a face.

Only the owner sets `Status: filled`.

Unknown public use stays off public copy and off Proof.

A Proof metric needs definition, unit, period, source, and a public yes.

Else it stays on the interview.
