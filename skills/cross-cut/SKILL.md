---
name: cross-cut
description: "Split one long source into distinct pieces that share its facts and voice. Use for an interview, a letter, or a talk. It does not add new claims."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

One source, separate pieces, the same facts. Pick the shape before the sentences: LinkedIn (fold, then an ask), X (one claim), letter (one takeaway), spoken script (the payoff has to be on screen), native article (the argument, no new proof). A number that appears twice must match. If the source cannot carry a shape, skip that shape. Add no claim the source does not already make.
