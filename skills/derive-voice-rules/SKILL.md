---
name: derive-voice-rules
description: "Derive voice rules from the owner's own samples and edits. Use when Tone of Voice or brand.local/voice.md is thin. It does not import another person's voice."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Read `brand.local/identity.md`, `brand.local/voice.md`, and `vault/voice/`. If `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` is filled, read it so a reader phrase is not filed as the owner's. If the voice file is missing, copy `brand/voice.md` to `brand.local/voice.md`. Template prompts are not owner rules. If identity names a Tone of Voice page, point at it and do not contradict or paste it. If unread, say so. Voice rules go only in `brand.local/voice.md`. No formats file, second guide, index, or log.

Save a new sample in `vault/voice/` under its filename. If taken, add the date beside the older file. Do not summarize, commit, or fetch a public post as a stand-in. Do not invent a sample or a quote.

Beside each path, classify: written by the owner, a speech transcript, a ghostwritten post they approved, a draft they rejected, or external inspiration. An edit is preference. An engagement count is an outcome, not authorship. External inspiration is not a rule or example. A spoken habit is not a writing rule unless written samples show it. A rejected draft may add under Don't or Banned words, not a line to copy. An approved ghostwritten post records what they let stand. Only a later edit is preference evidence.

Use several samples when that writing has them. One is tentative, not a universal style. Name file count, types, and what they cannot prove. Observe openings, endings, rhythm, specificity, vocabulary, punctuation, paragraph length, and argument shape. A short quote needs a path.

Write into existing `brand.local/voice.md` sections. A cross-sample pattern is a rule. Keep an irregular habit samples show. Do not hard-code a dialect, slang quota, CTA frequency, numeric separator, or hook length unless samples repeat it. Struck words go under Banned words.

Calibration draft only when asked. Read `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` first. Stop if missing or blank. Use a source they supplied and marked public. A private sample may inform rules and stay out of the passage. Add no unstated fact. Put it in `work/voice-sample.md`. File the offered line, the kept line, and only rules the edit supports. Do not mark an imitation approved. Leave `Status: blank` until they say the rules are theirs. Show the guide before treating it as filled.

If there are no samples, collect them. If they still want a passage, write a labeled neutral draft and say it is not their voice. Do not claim a voice from a biography or `brand.local/identity.md` alone. Hand back the guide. This skill does not post, send, or spend.
