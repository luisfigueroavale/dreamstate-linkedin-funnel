# Classify

Beside each path, classify: written by the owner, a speech transcript, a ghostwritten post they approved, a draft they rejected, or external inspiration.

An edit is preference. An engagement count is an outcome, not authorship. External inspiration is not a rule or example.

A spoken habit is not a writing rule unless written samples show it. A rejected draft may add under Don't or Banned words, not a line to copy.

An approved ghostwritten post records what they let stand. Only a later edit is preference evidence.
