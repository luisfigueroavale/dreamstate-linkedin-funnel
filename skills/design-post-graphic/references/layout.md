# Layout

Spacing, hierarchy, and relationships do the work. Ban diagrams inside quotations. Do not stamp a card per slide. Use HTML text, not flat images or paths. Keep labels apart. Connectors do not cross or cover type. Keep contrast readable. Use a vertical rail or left-to-right row. If connectors cross, change the layout and render again.

Draw process plates or SVG. Keep product screenshots whole. Owner photos stay unchanged. Use fonts the asset README lists. Confirm faces are on screen.
