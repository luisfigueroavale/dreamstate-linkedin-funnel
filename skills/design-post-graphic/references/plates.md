# Plates

Teaching frames use labels people can follow. Each plate states a label and a fact true at that step, in up to three lines under the name. Number fewer, larger plates in one nameplate style. On overflow, cut a line or add a slide rather than shrink type.

The post needs a headline, earned points, and attribution. Keep lines as written. Smoother wording is a rewrite. Leave it out. The whole post stays off the frame. Keep the sequence, the figures, and the limits that change meaning. Add no figure the source lacks. Skip a screenshot you lack.

An instructional plate says what to do, or one fact the reader can act on. Start with a verb. Name the object: a folder, a command, or a weekday. A line that only describes does not belong. A quotation, an editorial line, and a proper name keep their grammar.
