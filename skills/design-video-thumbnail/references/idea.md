# Idea

Read the video, the reader, the title, and `brand.local`. Stop if a required brand file is blank.

Settle on one concrete idea the video can fulfill. That idea can be a contrast or an outcome the footage actually reaches.

Use a face or a screenshot only when that file was supplied. Do not generate a face. Do not invent a result screen or an endorsement. Do not stage a reaction the footage does not show.
