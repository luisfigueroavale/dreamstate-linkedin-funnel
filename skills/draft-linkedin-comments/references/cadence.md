# Cadence

## Cadence

Run twice on a weekday. Ask when their audience is actually on LinkedIn. Many use mid-morning. The second pass is mid-afternoon. Use their time zone. The window is 3 hours. The batch is 10 to 12 comments. Weekends are thin. Open the window back to the prior run's `fetched_at`. In the handover, say those posts are older.

They answer those themselves.
