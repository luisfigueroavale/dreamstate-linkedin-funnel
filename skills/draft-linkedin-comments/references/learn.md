# Learn

## Learn

If `work/draft-linkedin-comments/` already has a run, start from the newest file. Set each earlier draft against what they really posted. Verdicts are `posted as drafted`, `posted with edits`, and `not posted`. When their comments could not be read, the verdict is `unknown`. Zero posts from them on that run is a missed window.

That is not a verdict on the drafts.

Match the 19-digit id that follows `activity-` or `activity:`. Strip a leading @mention. Collapse whitespace. Ignore automated lines: a bare sent note, a "check your DMs" delivery, any link, a `utm_` tag, or the same line repeated word for word. With two drafts, name which one (A or B) landed closer.

Early rounds, while picks are still settling, write 2 drafts on each post. After two runs where one draft would have been the card they took most of the time, return to a single draft.

An edit becomes a voice rule in `brand.local/voice.md`, not a stored copy of their sentence. A pattern seen twice becomes a rule. Edits are the strongest voice signal. One line in the handover says what you learned. Recent comments of theirs are texture only.

Skip reasons a card may store: "Post too old", "Already said", "Wrong angle", "Too long", "Not how I talk", "Not my topic", "No reason".

If `brand.local/voice.md` has no comment rules yet, measure and ask before the first batch. Use the last 50 to 100 comments they left under other people's posts. Drop automated deliveries. Also drop replies they left on their own posts.

Record how many you read, the median word count, the share of 1 liners, the longest comment plus its topic, the usual opening, and the usual close. Until that measurement exists, follow the default shape and mention that in the handover. Two stops before the first batch: show the measured shape and wait, then show the watchlist and wait.
