## Receipt

Check who wrote the post before the draft. Vault notes and Company Context say whether this person is already known. A known person can be addressed by name, in a warmer register. An unknown person's comment has to stand on the post alone. Private exchanges stay out of the public comment.

A story enters only as a direct parallel. If it needs a bridge, it is the wrong story. Use one in 2 to 4 comments out of 10. Say it the way they would over a coffee, and only with details the source actually gives. If that story showed up in the past 7 days, leave it out.

The rest of the batch reacts, adds know-how, answers, or asks.

Every fact comes from the post or from vault and Company Context. Every number comes from proof, in that wording, and it is the newest cleared figure. Cut a sentence about the owner that has no source, or turn it into a question.
