## Receipt

Look the post's author up before writing. Notes, plus lines already in vault or Company Context, say whether there is a shared history. No hit means no shared moment. Do not invent one. A person they know can be named, with more warmth. A stranger has to earn the comment alone. DMs, calls, and deals stay private.

A story enters only as a direct parallel. If it needs a bridge, it is the wrong story. Use one in 2 to 4 comments out of 10. Say it the way they would over a coffee, and only with details the source actually gives. If that story showed up in the past 7 days, leave it out.

The rest of the batch reacts, adds know-how, answers, or asks.

Every fact comes from the post or from vault and Company Context. Every number comes from proof, in that wording, and it is the newest cleared figure. Cut a sentence about the owner that has no source, or turn it into a question. No invented anecdote, feeling, client, number, or plan.

No claim that a product has a feature which is not live.
