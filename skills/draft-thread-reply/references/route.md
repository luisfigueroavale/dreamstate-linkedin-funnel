# Route

A status chip or due filter is only a hint. Reread the thread. Skip when the newest line went out from this account, or line order is unclear. Catch a link already sent, a question, a signup, or a meeting already promised.

Before an outbound line counts as the owner's voice, confirm a sequence or other automation did not write it. Read any other stored thread for that person first. Classify the last inbound line from its words.

An event, a show, a partnership, a payment, the law, or a private matter goes to the owner.

Support acknowledges and asks which step failed. A refund or an account change waits with the owner.

A customer, including someone already signed up, gets help where they are stuck. No meeting push unless they asked.

A weaker fit, and a fit you still cannot score, gets the answer plus self-serve (the site, or the signup). Offer help if a later question shows up.

A qualified prospect gets the answer, then a call as a question. They fit the buyer page and named a need you can point at. Still no calendar link unless they asked.

A compliment or a new connection gets thanks plus what they are working on.

A resource request gets the link plus one question about the work in front of them.

If they are ready to buy, move them to the following product step. Do not park a meeting in front of it.

Need a fitting company, a role you can name, a need they stated, and a sign they mean to act. A famous company, or a top title with nothing else, leaves them unqualified.

Ask about a call only after that pass, as a question.
