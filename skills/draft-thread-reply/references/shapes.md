# Shapes that fail

These shapes fail, composed from this thread:

- guided tour or demo lead

- freeing a quarter of an hour to show what shipped recently

- a call in order to go deeper

- more detail or a call, then pick whatever time suits them

- after a light note, grab a slot

- a sign-off that you help groups like theirs push the same problem further

- a booking link for any time they want

- a rough number locked behind a meeting

- a heap of material and which themes interest them

- a social note past two, three, or four short lines, or one combining updates, a booking link, and a request they try what you sell

- after signup, more than one ordinary sentence and one question, or product layers in jargon

- a numbered list or loose list that does not ask something clear

- a feature speech when how their setup works would move a live buyer

- replying to a thanks, a done, or a line that already ended the thread.

A booking link is allowed once they requested a conversation, or while you are setting a time. That closer as the default fails. A call itself is allowed.
