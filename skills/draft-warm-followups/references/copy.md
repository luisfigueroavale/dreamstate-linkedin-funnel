# Copy

Start with buyer fit and the exact public context.

Match what they did.

Keep the copy evidence-based, adaptable, and short.

Add no deadline or timing claim, and do not make a rate claim.

Use three steps or fewer.

Do not add a questionnaire.

Use the post's words for the topic, the comment's actual point, one experience you stand behind, and whether that problem is their work.

A relevant question beats a like.

Allow a file ask or product weighing only if it is true.

Do not write a reaction-only observation.

Waiting or a public follow-up can be right.

Do not say you saw a like.

Ask one question.

Do not make an offer.

Do not add a calendar link unless they asked to meet.

Do not add a side pitch off the file handoff.

Ask about today's work and the stuck point, and only what they shared.

If the offer addresses that problem, keep it proportionate: one relevant example, part of the asset, or one evidenced suggestion.

The conversation can pick an answer, the resource, one signup, a demonstration, or a call.

Stop on a decline or a stop request.

Reply stops stay on.

Replies go to `draft-thread-reply`.
