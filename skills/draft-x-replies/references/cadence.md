# Cadence

Ask when their targets are online. Run twice on those days. For a US audience, use that timezone's morning and its afternoon.

Each ordinary pass looks back 3 hours. Hand them somewhere from eight replies through fifteen. Their share of the work is about ten minutes. Keep the window short. People see the replies that land early.

On a day the owner posts, they answer the replies under that post themselves, during the first hour. This queue does not include those.

Speed pass, while they are at the keyboard: look back 1.5 hours, keep originals under 90 minutes, hand them 5 to 8, and run again every 20 to 30 minutes until they stop.

Any topic they can stand behind still has to clear politics, promos, and the other flags in `references/enter.md`. When junk terms exist, the term minimum still applies unless they marked this pass broad.

A thin window (a weekend, or their main audience asleep) keeps only posts from the last three hours, and a small page, then a later pass. Do not backfill with older posts.

Hand back two lines: the page path, the count, and the 2 or 3 to send first because they go stale fastest.
