# Draft

Read `brand/AGENTS.md`, `brand.local/`, `references/verdicts.md`, and `taste.json` before writing. Write one reply per post.

Every fact comes from the receipts, the Proof page, the voice file, or the post. Every number comes from the Proof page.

A reply is one thought to a peer: usually one or two short sentences, 70 to 160 characters, and never over 200.

Say it with contractions and plain words, in the texture already in `brand.local/voice.md` (how they laugh, whether they use emoji, lowercase, the words they use for people).

Do not write a windup sentence, a punchline sentence, or a triad of parallel beats. One receipt is enough.

Do not pile a story, a number, and a lesson into the same line, and do not show off a number nobody asked for. At most one question, and only after a concrete line.

The question is short, and the poster could answer it from experience.

Do not open with a compliment or use one as padding. Do not add a link, a hashtag, or a product plug.

When they have named the company, that name appears only when it is the receipt itself, and in at most one reply out of ten. If they have not named it, there is no name check.

Set `angle` to one of these seven names. `receipt`: the line is work they already do. `gap`: the post left out the piece they know. `tried`: they followed that advice, or they dropped it. `method`: the post never shows the how, and they have one.

`ask`: a short question sits after a concrete line. `pushback`: their own work gives them a reason to disagree. `joke`: the post is funny and they can add a true detail.

## Starting tastes

Starting tastes, kept until verdicts replace them:

Each reply carries one practice, tool, or result they have now. A pure opinion, an old story, or a plug is skipped.

Open on the hack or the result. A draft that is only a question rarely gets sent.

Meet the post's point first: agree by adding the how, answer the question they asked, or add a joke the owner can stand behind.

Under a hype post or a results post, match their energy in the owner's words, then one result or one trick if there is one.

Leave out setup tours: routines, repos, agent counts, subscriptions.

Prefer plain words. Leave out age, years, and team size unless the post asked.

Do not hang another reply on the same failure story.

Drafts under posts 15 to 60 minutes old are the ones that actually get sent. Drafts under posts older than 3 hours mostly do not.
