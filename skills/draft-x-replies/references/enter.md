# What enters

Candidates are original posts. Replies and reposts do not enter. A quote stays, scored on its own text.

Build the handle set from the watchlist priorities in play, plus the circle. A speed pass uses the reach set instead of the full watchlist. See `references/sets.md`.

Read `visibility.radar.inspiration_x_list` in handle batches of at most 100, newest stored first. Also take posts the owner pasted. Cap the scored list at 40.

Skip a post they already replied to (same post or same conversation) using the replies they sent in the last day. Skip anything that was already on the previous run's page.

One reply to a given account per day is enough. `replied_to_author_recently` at 1 or more leaves them out today.

People who replied to the owner in the last 24 hours, capped at 50, are the talker set.

Flags, any of which blocks a pick:

- Giveaway: ask to comment a code word, reply with a quoted word, repost-and-follow, or the word giveaway.

- Politics, drama, or tragedy. The politics match includes election, democrat, republican, and war.

- The whole post is a single link.

- Promo: airdrop, presale, a cashtag, crypto, web3, whitelist, token launch, bonding curve, escrow, telegram, sponsored, limited spots, final hour, hours left, jobs found today, dm me for, i help founders.

- Hiring: a job alert, now hiring, work mode, job type, apply here, remote jobs, or a pin-and-briefcase location line.

- A run of four or more styled unicode letters used as a fake headline.

- A campaign: the same three-word phrase, ignoring articles, pronouns, and short prepositions, from four or more accounts in one fetch.

Off-list posts (a paste, or a stored post whose author is not on the watchlist) also need a follower floor of 5,000, or 2,000 on a core-niche pass, and they need to touch at least two lanes.

A core-niche pass keeps a post only when it matches the focus lanes. Promo or politics drops them. Do not add a second copy of someone already on the watchlist.
