# Lists

Blank files in `references/` start at `Status: blank`. Stop and fill them with the owner before the first drafts. Ask only for what the pages and their past replies do not already show.

When they supply old replies, read at least 50, skip the automated ones, and write texture into `brand.local/voice.md` as rules. Do not paste those sentences into the rule list.

Pause twice: once after voice, receipts, and lanes, and again after the watchlist, the circle, and the reach list.

`references/watchlist.md` holds 50 to 150 accounts. Seed it with whoever already gets their replies, whoever they volunteer, and peers who post inside the lanes.

A row has a handle, a display name, a follower count, a lane, and notes. Priority is a digit from 1 to 3, or the word off. Off stays out of every pass. Their own handle stays out.

`references/lanes.md` holds 3 to 5 lanes, each a keyword pattern, plus the smaller set for a core-niche pass, plus the junk terms. A junk gate with no terms is off.

`references/receipts.md` holds four groups: habits and tools in use, figures they will say out loud, positions in their phrasing, and a blocked list of claims that are not true yet.

A reply must not use the blocked list. When a figure disagrees with the Proof page, the Proof page wins. Each line names its source. Refresh the file when a figure or a position changes.

`references/circle.md` is the circle. `references/reach.md` is the large-account list. `references/verdicts.md` is the lesson log. Runs and `taste.json` go under `work/draft-x-replies/`, which is gitignored.
