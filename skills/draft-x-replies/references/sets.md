# Circle and reach

About 10 accounts, engaged on every run, so the name becomes familiar and they answer. Pick them with the owner from a 7-day look: they post often in the lanes, they get real engagement, and they reply to other people.

Under the circle heading, write one line with the handle, the name, the follower count, and why. Lines under Alternates are ignored. Move a line up to swap someone in.

Circle posts stay eligible out to 6 hours. They receive a 1.6 boost. The page marks them with a circle chip.

The taste ledger does not drop them, and the half-score for repeated skips does not apply. In a speed pass they stay in even under 50,000 followers.

If they were missing from the watchlist, treat them as on it for that pass. When anyone in the circle posted inside the window, the batch includes at least one reply to them.

`references/reach.md` is for the speed pass: active accounts at or above 50,000 followers, in or near the lanes, on topics where the owner can add a real line. Confirm the follower count before a name goes in.

Skip a political feed, a crypto feed, an account that only posts tool roundups, and a company account with no back-and-forth. Write one line with the handle and the follower count.

The speed set is that file, plus the circle, plus watchlist rows already at 50,000 followers. If the file is still blank, speed uses the circle and those watchlist rows.
