# Reading order

State the reading order before you place a card: what comes in, what changes, what the reader leaves with. Name the trigger, the input, the work, each decision, the output, the exception, and any feedback.

A split lands on a card at every exit. An arrow is a dependency you can point to. A lane names who owns the work. A filter is a container that is wider where more enters and tighter where fewer remain.

A set of pillars or tests is a taxonomy. Top, middle, and bottom name jobs of a piece. A reader can stop at one band.

When the map is about people who reacted, keep separate stations for the capture, the company fit, the person fit, the route, the draft, the review, and the send. Set `station` to `capture`, `company-fit`, `person-fit`, `route`, `draft`, `review`, or `send`.

Date a snapshot. Mark a station that is only proposed. A like is not intent. A reaction grants no permission and completes no sale. Do not draw a diamond that says they will buy because they reacted. A card on the page does not run the station, enroll anyone, or send.
