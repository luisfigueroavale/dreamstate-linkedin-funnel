# Edit a long video

Shape a long recording as a designed opener plus the screen recording. Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when

- Reach for it when a straight trim cannot carry the opening pictures.

- A body that is only a trim goes through `trim-talking-head-takes`.

- It does not upload.

## Steps

1. This brief and this footage choose the treatment (`references/shape.md`).

2. Plan graphic beats from the transcript when graphics cover the whole file (`references/shape.md`).

3. Transcribe (`references/transcript.md`).

4. Inspect (`references/transcript.md`).

5. Write the source-range list (`references/transcript.md`).

6. Build (`references/opener.md`).

7. Hold (`references/opener.md`).

8. Build that opener as `DeskTimeline` (`references/build.md`). Capture calls `__seek`.

9. Cut the screen-recording body by source range only (`references/body.md`).

10. Set (`references/body.md`).

11. Assemble (`references/build.md`).

12. Encode (`references/build.md`).

13. Keep (`references/build.md`).
## Dreamstate tools

None. Local files only.

## Stop rules

- A direction for this recording outranks a preset saved from an earlier job.

- Do not draw a fake card.

- A counter may climb only as far as the real value.

- Do not stamp a rounder caption over it.

- When the speech and a receipt disagree, put the receipt on screen and tell the owner.

- Do not use captions that are large and all the same, slabs that crowd the frame, wipes that look cheap, or transitions that bounce.

- Do not fade the overlay parent.

- If that parent sits below opacity 1, the blur switches off and the frost arrives late.

- Do not let a talking stretch run past about 7 seconds with no visual change.

- That stretch gets no captions, no zooms, no overlays, and no chapter breaks.

- Fix timing only by cutting source ranges.

- No music unless they asked.

- Do not stamp a click on a repeating grid.

- CSS does not animate.

- A descendant forced to stay visible can outlive the parent's timer and leave a panel up.

- Do not vendor a font.

- Title, description, thumbnail, and upload are other skills.

- Do not upload.
