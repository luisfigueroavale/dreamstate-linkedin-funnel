# Body and sound

The usual body is the screen recording, picture only. When they asked for that, remove unwanted time and stutters, and add nothing else. That stretch gets no captions, no zooms, no overlays, and no chapter breaks. Fix timing only by cutting source ranges.

Sound follows events the viewer can see. Use few quiet cues. Add them only if they asked for sound design, and tie each one to a clear motion: a panel coming in, a landing, or a reveal.

Keep their voice. Do not stamp a click on a repeating grid. No music unless they asked. Set the mix by ear against the words, then measure the export.
