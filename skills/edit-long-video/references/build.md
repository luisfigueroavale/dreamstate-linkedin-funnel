# Build and encode

Build the opener as an editable motion scene in HTML. Motion is `DeskTimeline`, and capture calls `__seek`. Hide every element until its entrance, and have each one gone before the last opener frame, so the join into the body shows no leftover.

Also inspect descendants. Run the motion check, render, and look at a timed contact sheet plus the encoded opener before assembly.

Review the opening frames, each graphic as it enters and as it exits, the last opener frame, and the joins. A structural pass will miss a graphic that lingers past its beat.

Assemble the rendered opener, the cut body, and the close. Check picture and sound on every cut. Inspect the camera-to-screen handoff frame by frame, and do the same for the closing cut.

Encode H.264 video and AAC audio at the frame rate of the recording. Loudness about -14 LUFS integrated and true peak at or under -1.5 dBTP. Decode the whole file. Confirm duration and streams. Review a contact sheet of that finished file.

`brand.local` is required for titles and overlays, not for the cuts. Fonts come from files listed there. If none are listed, say you are on the system face. Keep the editable scene, the cut list, and the check notes beside the file. Do not upload.
