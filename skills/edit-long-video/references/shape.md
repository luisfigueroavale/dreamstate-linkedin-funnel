# Shape

Make one finished file from the recording they gave you. This brief and this footage choose the treatment. A direction for this recording outranks a preset saved from an earlier job.

The usual shape is a designed opener of the speaker, then the screen recording.

If they want graphics on the whole file, plan those beats from the transcript: a chapter card where the speech changes section, a short idea plate on a flat ground or a frosted ground, a spotlight on the interface they name, and a hard reframe where they face the camera.

Review each beat you planned. Leave a pause empty when it does not need a graphic.
