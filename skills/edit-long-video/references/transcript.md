# Transcript

Transcribe with a time on every word. Mark the hook, every claim that deserves a picture, the first frame of the demonstration, and the closing transition. Start on the spoken hook. Cut the dead air and the false starts at the top. For each removal, record the source in-point, the source out-point, and why it goes.

Watch the picture and listen at every join. Keep a pause when the viewer still has to follow the screen. Before you build, inspect the recording, the transcript, the frame rate, and the audio, and write that source-range list.
