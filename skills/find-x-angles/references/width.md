# How wide

A pasted post or a narrow question is a lookup. Open that post and only the context the question needs. Do not widen it into a scan.

A discovery request starts with two or three different queries and a short page. From the pillars you can draft up to about ten seeds (the problem in the reader's words, a tool in use, an outcome they want, a failure, a company decision). Do not fire the whole list.

Add a query only when the pages you have are noisy, thin, or off the reader. Widen a thin query. Narrow one that comes back as generic comment. A noise exclusion, such as giveaways, waits until you actually see that noise. Do not ban every resource offer.

The default window is the last 7 days. Say the window you used. Older material comes from a file they supplied or from the browser. Do not claim a thread is complete when the parent is outside that window or the page stopped early.
