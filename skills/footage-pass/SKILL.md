name: footage-pass
description: "Review a supplied video from a transcript or a silence map and write the keeps. Use before a cut. It does not generate pictures of the speaker."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

From their transcript, or from a silence map when they have no transcript, write keeps as in-point and out-point. `brand.local` matters when you add a title or an overlay. It does not matter when you only mark cuts. Do not generate a picture of the speaker. A still, if you need one, is a frame of their footage.
