---
name: frame-review
description: "Review a rendered frame at full size and phone width, and a motion filmstrip when one exists. Use after frame-build. The owner is the last reviewer."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Five scores: meaning, content fit (steps, numbers, and caveats match the source), identity, craft, and delivery. Label the evidence source only, rendered, or rendered and inspected. Check every slide, not a sample, at full size and at 390px wide. When the page moves, review the frames `__seek` produces, including the last one. A script exit is not a review. The owner decides last.
