# Write one issue

One takeaway a reader can use. Sections follow the source. A story that is not in the source stays out. The close is one next step from the brief, or none.

Save `vault/posts/<slug>-letter.md`. This is not a sequence and not a send. If they later want it as email, that is `warm-cadence` or a new sequence, with its own yes.
