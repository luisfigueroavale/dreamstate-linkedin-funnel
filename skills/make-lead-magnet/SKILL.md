# Make a lead magnet

Turn one reader job into a folder they can finish without the owner. Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when
- The owner asks for a lead magnet: a calculator, template, repo, checklist, or guide.
- Not for delivery (`send-lead-magnet`) or the launch post (`write-linkedin-post`, `write-x-post`).

## Steps
1. Read `audience/AGENTS.md`, `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`, and `brand.local/identity.md`. Stop if one is missing or `Status: blank`.
2. Set the sources: the follower file is the reader, the Ideal Customer page is the offer, and the Proof page is the only place claims come from. Name each page and its revision.
3. Choose the one job the reader does next: decide, calculate, configure a workflow, or finish a draft. Write `promise.md` as one sentence naming what they hold at the end.
4. Pick the format that fits the job (`references/files.md`, "Format").
5. Copy `magnets/example/` to `magnets/<slug>/` and rewrite every line. Fill the five files as `references/files.md` specifies.
6. Write `README.md` so a stranger reaches the first result, then checks it: bad input, a skipped requirement, and what failure looks like.
7. Run the stranger test in `references/checks.md`. You are done when one result is reachable with the folder alone.
8. Hand over the folder, confirm each check passed, and say that nothing was sent.

## Dreamstate tools
None. Local files only. Tracked links and delivery belong to `send-lead-magnet`.

## Stop rules
- Follow the skill-frame writing rules.
- Proof is unread: use no number and no result. Never use a figure from another company's customers.
- Paste no Company Context, Ideal Customer, Proof, or follower text into the folder.
- No address is published yet: write "unknown". Never invent a URL or use a short link.
- No send, post, enroll, keyword arm, or outside board. Publishing needs its own yes.
- Call a preview a preview and a scaffold a scaffold. Neither is a working integration.
- The launch post goes to `write-linkedin-post` or `write-x-post` once the folder exists. Do not send it.
