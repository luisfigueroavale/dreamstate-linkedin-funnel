---
name: opening-line
description: "Write or compare LinkedIn opening lines in the owner's voice, inside the feed fold. Use for hook options or a first-line edit. It does not write the rest of the post."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Pick one family: a result the source states, a test or a comparison, a bare claim, news tied to a result, a confession tied to a change, the stake, before then after, or a receipt stated as the promise. House budgets, not a platform cap: first line about 40–60 characters, the essential opening inside 140, the fold you inspect at 210 with `scripts/count-fold.mjs`, the whole post inside 3000. Reject an opener that says a crowd is already doing the thing and almost nobody will say the rest. `social.content.generate_hooks` is free (`user_request` and `platform` required; dry_run is allowed). `social.draft_score` is a free read (`text` required; sending dry_run executes the read). No em dash. No invented number. Offer options. The owner picks.
