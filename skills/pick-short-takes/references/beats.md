# Beats

For each beat record the source file, in-point, out-point, and why that range.

Drop false starts.

A retake can sit inside a phrase.

Inspect at word level.

Speech-to-text times are a start.

Listen around every cut so a consonant or a breath is not clipped.

Keep each word boundary whole, and keep the speech meaning.

Remove silence inside a take only when the pace gets better.

Keep a pause they meant.

Drop dead air that sits between attempts.
