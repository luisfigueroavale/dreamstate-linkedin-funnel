# Map the pillars

Read the Ideal Customer page, Proof, and `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`, plus `brand.local/identity.md`. Write `vault/notes/pillars.md` as a list. Each pillar has: name, reader, job (attention, trust, or ask), and the proof it may use. A pillar with no proof source is marked "no proof yet" and may not carry a result.

Three to five pillars. Do not add a pillar to look complete. Show the file and wait before replacing an existing map.
