Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

One pillar, one job, in `vault/notes/pillars.md`. The jobs are defined in `vault/notes/funnel-stages.md`: reach (the file has to be seen), belief (the argument changes a mind), signup (the resource post asks them to take the file). Name the reader from the filled follower file, the proof that pillar may use, and the close that job allows. A reach pillar does not end on a booking link. Leave unknown sections empty. Do not draft the posts.
