# Monday

On Monday the plan gets two more sections above the day.

## The week

Three lines: the angles moving in the owner's space this week, with source links and counts, and the pillar each one serves. Pull them from the same `find-x-angles` read.

## Voice notes to record

Three prompts the owner can answer out loud in a minute or two each. Each prompt asks for something only the owner has: a decision they made this week, a mistake and what they changed, a question a customer asked and how they answered it. Tie each prompt to one pillar and one stage (reach, belief, or signup).

When the owner sends a recording or its transcript back, follow `references/voice-note.md`.
