# Plan a content week

It points at the stage skills and orders one week from a sourced idea. Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when

- They name an idea, want pieces from a recording, hand over a file or a live link, or ask to split one source.

- Not for scheduling, uploading, or publishing without a yes on that exact item.

## Steps

1. Stop if `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` is missing or blank (`references/rules.md`).

2. Read `docs/plan-content-week.md`, then copy `vault/week/brief.md` to `vault/week/<date>.md` and fill every line (`references/rules.md`).

3. Enter at the stage the work is already in, and do not restart at 0 to look thorough (`references/enter.md`).

4. Keep the standing rules before any draft (`references/rules.md`). The idea is theirs.

5. Map the trunk and the three roots (`references/roots.md`).

6. Bring four scans back in stage 0, and do not turn them into the pick (`references/stage-0.md`).

7. Read LinkedIn outliers with `visibility.radar.inspiration_linkedin_list` (`references/stage-0.md`). Sending dry_run executes that read.

8. Read moving posts through `find-x-angles`, which reads `visibility.radar.inspiration_x_list` (`references/stage-0.md`).

9. Write stage 1 from the picked idea, and ask only the release day, and their verdict when the recording is a review (`references/stage-1.md`).

10. Reuse rows on `vault/board.md`, move a line with `update-content-board`, and set a day mark with `social.content.plan` (`references/stage-2.md`). That mark is not a schedule.

11. Leave the recording to them in stage 3 (`references/stage-3.md`). You do not record for them.

12. Package in stage 4, and hand off with `publish-youtube-video` only after a yes on that exact video (`references/stage-4.md`).

13. Draft stage 5 from the letter first (`references/stage-5.md`).

14. Queue a piece with `social.content.schedule` only after yes on that text, that media, and that time (`references/stage-6.md`).

15. Dispatch that version with `social.content.approve` (`references/stage-6.md`).

16. Convert engagers in stage 7 (`references/stage-7.md`). A view count does not finish the week.

17. Store a watcher with `tables.profile_watcher.create` after yes (`references/stage-7.md`).

18. Collect engagers with `visibility.linkedin.engagement_harvest` through `score-post-engagers` (`references/stage-7.md`).

19. Close in stage 8 from `docs/plan-content-week.md`, and fetch each id before you run it (`references/stage-8.md`).

20. After each stage, name the file or id that came back and the next stage (`references/handoff.md`).

21. Call `whoami` before the first Dreamstate write in the session (`references/rules.md`).

22. Run `tools_get` before `tools_run` (`references/rules.md`).

## Stop rules

- Follow the skill-frame writing rules.

- A missing or blank `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` stops the week.

- It points at the stage skills and does not schedule, upload, or publish.

- Do not redo that skill's steps here.

- Do not restart at 0 to look thorough.

- Do not draft a claim they have not sourced.

- Ask only what they alone can decide.

- Do not pick for them.

- Do not invent the week's idea.

- A title, a thumbnail, and the letter may claim only what the recording shows.

- Drop a number the recording will not show.

- A number comes from the Proof page, or from a source they supplied.

- Do not shorten a video link. A short link is treated as spam and as phishing.

- Do not add a second scale.

- Nothing is posted, scheduled, sent, enrolled, or uploaded without a yes on that exact item.

- Call `whoami` before the first Dreamstate write in the session.

- `tools_get` before `tools_run`.

- Do not turn the four scans into the pick.

- `choose-next-video` does not call a video-host API.

- Do not assume a browser ranking plugin.

- Do not invent the citation.

- A new resource is `make-lead-magnet`, and only if they ask.

- A second planning database is not used.

- `social.content.plan` does not publish, and that mark is not a schedule.

- You do not record for them.

- Packaging is not that yes.

- Privacy stays private until they name otherwise.

- Do not copy a connection from another project.

- This desk does not ship a transcription service.

- Do not mine the transcript again to invent a fresh argument for every row.

- Do not add a 3D scene library.

- Do not also arm a second send for the same piece.

- `write-newsletter-issue` does not send mail.

- An idea post never becomes a second post on a day that already has one.

- The unschedule call does not retract a live post.

- Do not start that sequence in the same step as sending the file.

- Do not open a workflow, a cohort, or a send until they say yes.

- `draft-linkedin-comments` and `draft-x-replies` do not post on their own.

- A customer win enters the next brief only with permission, and only with a number already cleared.

- Do not open a second log.

- Do not guess a version and retry.

- Re-read the artifact.

- Do not paste a walkthrough link.

- Do not create a second planning database.

- Do not upload to a video host.

- Do not install a ranking plugin, a paid social radar, or a 3D scene library.

- Do not run another product's installer.

- This repo is already the desk.

- Sending dry_run to `visibility.radar.inspiration_linkedin_list` executes that read.

- `tables.profile_watcher.create` stores the watcher and does not write post rows.
