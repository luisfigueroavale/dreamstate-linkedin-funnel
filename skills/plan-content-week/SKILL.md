Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

This skill is the order for one week. Name the stage skill and let it do the work. Do not redo that skill's steps here. Read `docs/plan-content-week.md` for the day table and the link rules. Copy `vault/week/brief.md` to `vault/week/<date>.md` and fill every line.

One idea becomes one long recording. The other pieces reuse that recording. The stage skills draft. The owner picks the idea, records, reads, and edits. Once the desk is running, batch that into one content day: the idea, the recording, and reading the drafts. Leave the other days for customers. Volume is reuse of this recording, not a second argument.

Use this when they name an idea, want pieces from a recording, hand over a file or a live link, or ask to split one source. A missing or blank `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` stops the week.

## Where to enter

Start at the stage the work is already in. Do not restart at 0 to look thorough.

| What they handed you | Start |
|---|---|
| No idea yet | 0 |
| An idea, or a release day | 1. Packaging before the outline |
| A raw file | 4 |
| A live link | 5 |

If the brief has no piece list yet, write that list before any draft. Do not draft a claim they have not sourced. Ask only what they alone can decide.

## The nine stages

| Stage | What it is | Finished when | They decide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Find candidates | A slate of three to five | The pick |
| 1 | Brief | `vault/week/<date>.md` filled | Release day; their verdict when the recording is a review |
| 2 | Board | Rows on the brief and `vault/board.md` | Only a day that two pieces both want |
| 3 | Record | The file they record | What the camera shows |
| 4 | Package | Folder ready for them to upload | Title, thumbnail, and the upload |
| 5 | Draft | Copy on each row | Edits, and which lines stand alone |
| 6 | Schedule | Armed only after yes | Yes on that exact item |
| 7 | Convert | Signals, fit, the file delivered, reply drafts | Any send they have not already allowed |
| 8 | Close | Results on the brief | The next idea, and what to run again |

## Standing rules

The idea is theirs. Offer three to five candidates. Do not pick for them. Do not invent the week's idea.

A title, a thumbnail, and the letter may claim only what the recording shows. A number comes from the Proof page, or from a source they supplied. Drop a number the recording will not show.

Voice is `brand.local/voice.md`. If they call a line generic or machine-made, rewrite that section from words they have already used.

A video link is the full https URL. Do not shorten it. A short link is treated as spam and as phishing. When a reply points at a chapter, append `&t=<seconds>s` so the link opens there.

Every link to their own site carries UTM. Source is the platform: `linkedin`, `x`, `youtube`, or `newsletter`. The campaign is one short slug for the whole week, written on the brief, and copied onto every link, so a signup can be tied to this week.

Use one parent row for the recording. Every other piece is a row on that week, with a status, a platform, and a date. Status is Idea, Drafting, Review, Published, or Backlog. Do not open a pile of unrelated rows.

Date every row from release day D. The letter and the X essay go on D. The rest follows `docs/plan-content-week.md`.

The week's posts are text, pictures, and graphics. The finished recording is posted on LinkedIn and on X only as the native file.

Set the funnel job before you draft. It chooses the hook and the close. The jobs are reach, belief, and signup, defined in `vault/notes/funnel-stages.md`. Do not add a second scale.

The long recording is the library. The letter is the list they own. X speaks to people who build. LinkedIn speaks to people who buy.

Drafting is not publishing. Nothing is posted, scheduled, sent, enrolled, or uploaded without a yes on that exact item. `tools_get` before `tools_run`. Call `whoami` before the first Dreamstate write in the session.

## Trunk and three roots

The live recording is the trunk: the file, the argument, and the transcript. The trunk row has no funnel job.

| Root | Reuses | Pieces | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, the file | The recording itself | Native file on LinkedIn, native file on X | Reach |
| 2, the argument | The letter | Letter, X essay, lesson, resource post | Belief, except the resource post, which is signup |
| 3, the transcript | Lines that stand alone | One chapter, a picture, a proof, an opinion, a short post | Reach, except a picture or a proof, which is belief |

From D: the recording, the letter, the X essay, and the short X note that it is up. The LinkedIn native file and the lesson land that same week, on days they still have open. The resource post is D+7. Idea posts take open days after that. Anything that does not earn a day waits in Backlog. At the close, a winner may be posted again on the other platform.

The letter exists before any piece that reuses its argument. One LinkedIn post a day.

## Stage 0

Bring four scans back. Do not turn them into the pick.

| Scan | Where | Bring back |
|---|---|---|
| Last week | The last brief's results | Winners, the sharpest questions, customer wins they allowed, calls |
| Long video | `choose-next-video` | Ideas with demand visible now, tied to the outcome this reader pays for, angle not closed yet |
| X | `find-x-angles`, which reads `visibility.radar.inspiration_x_list` | Posts in the niche that are already moving |
| LinkedIn | Posts they saved or pasted, or `visibility.radar.inspiration_linkedin_list` | Outlier posts and the hook. That read is free. Sending dry_run executes it |

`choose-next-video` uses evidence they supply. It does not call a video-host API. Do not assume a browser ranking plugin.

Each slate line carries four fields: why it is hot (the outlier, or numbers they actually have), which pillar in `vault/notes/pillars.md` it belongs to, which job it serves, and a receipt they can put on camera. Only a line that passes `choose-next-video` is a recording candidate. The rest may still feed LinkedIn or X. Prefer a topic with more demand than supply. A topic only they find interesting is the miss to avoid.

## Stage 1

1. Take the picked idea in their words. Use one pillar and one job. Ground it in `brand.local/` and `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`.
2. For stance, read what they already published on this. The recording extends it, or it names the change.
3. For facts, verify product claims, model claims, and market claims where they were published, and write the date beside each one. `write-research-brief` does that read. Do not invent the citation.
4. For the worked example and the resource, prefer what they can show, and a file that already exists. A new resource is `make-lead-magnet`, and only if they ask.
5. Packaging before the outline. `write-video-titles` writes two titles and two thumbnail directions. A structure counts as checked outside the niche only when `choose-next-video` says so. Otherwise it stays a hypothesis. The outline then has to make that promise true.
6. For the outline, `write-video-script` writes the opening minute word for word (that skill's gate on the first minute), then chapters. Each chapter says what is on screen and which claim it makes true. One chapter answers one objection a buyer raises, so a later reply can point at it. Use `write-video-script` for a full script when that helps.
7. Write the brief from `vault/week/brief.md`.

Ask only the release day, and their verdict when the recording is a review. Decide the rest and list it under Still open.

## Stage 2

Read `vault/board.md` and the brief for dates around the release, and for the same topic. Reuse a row that is already there.

This desk keeps one board, `vault/board.md`. Dreamstate holds the drafts. A second planning database would split status, so it is not used. Put the dated plan on the brief, and say it in chat.

`update-content-board` moves a line when status changes. `social.content.plan` sets a day mark on an existing draft and does not publish. That mark is not a schedule.

Default rows are the day table in `docs/plan-content-week.md`. Add a micro post, a chapter thread, or a repost of a winner only when they pick it. Keep the week near eight rows, not thirty. Other X posts are planned with them in `plan-x-week`.

## Stage 3

They record from the outline. They decide what the camera shows. You do not record for them.

Remind them: a clear screen, accounts they are willing to show, no client material in frame, and one rehearsal before the real take.

## Stage 4

| # | Step | Skill | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cut | `trim-talking-head-takes`, or `edit-long-video` when they want a designed open | False starts, empty gaps, and the leftover end of a screen demo are gone, and that skill's check passed |
| 2 | Transcript | The transcript they supply. `cut-raw-footage` can read it | Chapter times come from the finished cut, not the raw file |
| 3 | Titles | `write-video-titles` | Both titles are still true of the cut |
| 4 | Description | `write-video-description` | A block they can paste, with chapters |
| 5 | Thumbnail | `design-video-thumbnail` | Both sizes that skill names |
| 6 | Hand off | `publish-youtube-video` | That skill publishes after a yes on that exact video |

`publish-youtube-video` publishes on the connected YouTube account. Packaging is not that yes. Privacy stays private until they name otherwise. Do not copy a connection from another project. This desk does not ship a transcription service.

## Stage 5

Write the letter first with `write-newsletter-issue`. It is the argument the other pieces reuse. Then `write-x-article` from that letter, then `write-x-post` for the short note that the recording is up, then the LinkedIn posts. Do not mine the transcript again to invent a fresh argument for every row.

LinkedIn copy goes through `write-linkedin-post` and `write-opening-lines`. On LinkedIn the URL goes in the comment under the post, and the post tells the reader to look there. A LinkedIn article is optional, and only if they ask: `adapt-essay-for-linkedin`.

Root 3 is the harvest. Read the finished-cut transcript and tag each idea, in this order: take, line, receipt, strong claim, proof moment, framework, chapter. Ask whether it can stand without the recording. If yes, use one chapter as its own post, a picture (`design-post-graphic` or `draw-process-map`), a proof, an opinion, or a short X post planned in `plan-x-week`. If no, put it on Backlog. `split-long-source` helps the split. Pictures are HTML. Do not add a 3D scene library.

## Stage 6

Queue a piece on its day only after yes on that text, that media, and that time. `social.content.schedule` needs `expected_revision`, `scheduled_at`, and `artifact_id`. Dispatch still needs `social.content.approve` on that version. If they set the time inside the host by hand, wait for that yes too, and do not also arm a second send for the same piece.

The letter stays a draft until they approve it. `write-newsletter-issue` does not send mail. Status stays Drafting until the piece is live, then Published.

One LinkedIn post a day. An idea post takes an open day. It never becomes a second post on a day that already has one.

To pull an armed send back, use the unschedule call in stage 8. It does not retract a live post.

## Stage 7

A view count does not finish the week. An engager becomes a signal. The people who fit become a conversation.

Stage 7 is `score-post-engagers`, then `send-lead-magnet`, `draft-warm-followups`, or `draft-thread-reply`. A watcher on profiles they already named is `tables.profile_watcher.create` (`profile_urls`, `preview_hash`, `approved: true`, `table_id`) after yes. That call stores the watcher and does not write post rows. `score-post-engagers` collects the engagers with `visibility.linkedin.engagement_harvest` and scores company and person apart.

Someone who comments the keyword on the resource post gets the resource and nothing else, through `send-lead-magnet`. People who pass both scores can get a warm sequence from `draft-warm-followups`: capped, stopped when they reply, asking what they are working on. Do not start that sequence in the same step as sending the file. Replies are drafted in `draft-thread-reply`. The owner sends. For an objection, send the chapter that meets it. Use a full https URL and open it on that chapter with `&t=<seconds>s`.

Do not open a workflow, a cohort, or a send until they say yes. Between recordings, public replies on other people's posts are `draft-linkedin-comments` and `draft-x-replies`. Those skills do not post on their own.

## Stage 8

When a piece goes live, set its status to Published on the board and on the brief. Rank what shipped this week and anything older that is still in market. Keep the best one or two as candidates to post again on the other platform, and as input to the next recording. A customer win enters the next brief only with permission, and only with a number already cleared. Bring the winners and the sharpest questions when you ask what they want to make next. They still choose it.

On the brief, write the public links, any reply the tools returned, any signup they can show, how long the week took, which rows won, and what they chose to make next. Do not open a second log.

The close calls are in `docs/plan-content-week.md`. Fetch each id before you run it. Re-read the artifact. Do not guess a version and retry.

## Hand-off

After each stage, say which file or id came back, and which stage is next. Update the brief's titles, the description, the thumbnail paths, the dates, and the links before you stop.

## Left out

Do not paste a walkthrough link. Do not create a second planning database. Do not upload to a video host. Do not install a ranking plugin, a paid social radar, or a 3D scene library. Do not run another product's installer. This repo is already the desk.
