# Rules

Order one week from a sourced idea: a slate when they have none, then the brief, the recording, the letter, and the X and LinkedIn pieces.

This skill is the order for one week. Name the stage skill and let it do the work. Read `docs/plan-content-week.md` for the day table and the link rules. Copy `vault/week/brief.md` to `vault/week/<date>.md` and fill every line.

One idea becomes one long recording. The other pieces reuse that recording. The stage skills draft. The owner picks the idea, records, reads, and edits. Once the desk is running, batch that into one content day: the idea, the recording, and reading the drafts. Leave the other days for customers.

Volume is reuse of this recording, not a second argument.

Use this when they name an idea, want pieces from a recording, hand over a file or a live link, or ask to split one source.

## Standing rules

Offer three to five candidates.

Voice is `brand.local/voice.md`. If they call a line generic or machine-made, rewrite that section from words they have already used.

A video link is the full https URL. Do not shorten it. When a reply points at a chapter, append `&t=<seconds>s` so the link opens there.

Every link to their own site carries UTM. Source is the platform: `linkedin`, `x`, `youtube`, or `newsletter`. The campaign is one short slug for the whole week, written on the brief, and copied onto every link, so a signup can be tied to this week.

Use one parent row for the recording. Every other piece is a row on that week, with a status, a platform, and a date. Status is Idea, Drafting, Review, Published, or Backlog. Do not open a pile of unrelated rows.

Date every row from release day D. The letter and the X essay go on D. The rest follows `docs/plan-content-week.md`.

The week's posts are text, pictures, and graphics. The finished recording is posted on LinkedIn and on X only as the native file.

Set the funnel job before you draft. It chooses the hook and the close. The jobs are reach, belief, and signup, defined in `vault/notes/funnel-stages.md`.

The long recording is the library. The letter is the list they own. X speaks to people who build. LinkedIn speaks to people who buy.

Drafting is not publishing. `tools_get` before `tools_run`.
