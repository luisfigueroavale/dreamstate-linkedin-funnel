# Stage 0

## Stage 0

Bring four scans back.

| Scan | Where | Bring back |
|---|---|---|
| Last week | The last brief's results | Winners, the sharpest questions, customer wins they allowed, calls |
| Long video | `choose-next-video` | Ideas with demand visible now, tied to the outcome this reader pays for, angle not closed yet |
| X | `find-x-angles`, which reads `visibility.radar.inspiration_x_list` | Posts in the niche that are already moving |
| LinkedIn | Posts they saved or pasted, or `visibility.radar.inspiration_linkedin_list` | Outlier posts and the hook. That read is free. Sending dry_run executes it |

`choose-next-video` uses evidence they supply.

Each slate line carries four fields: why it is hot (the outlier, or numbers they actually have), which pillar in `vault/notes/pillars.md` it belongs to, which job it serves, and a receipt they can put on camera. Only a line that passes `choose-next-video` is a recording candidate. The rest may still feed LinkedIn or X.

Prefer a topic with more demand than supply. A topic only they find interesting is the miss to avoid.

## The bar on both feeds

An X or LinkedIn row counts as inspiration only when it clears `docs/viral-bar.md`. On LinkedIn read reactions as likes and impressions as views: 200 or more reactions, or 10,000 or more impressions when the read returned them. Rank first the posts at 5 to 10 times their author's average, computed from that author's recent posts with `skills/find-x-angles/scripts/viral_bar.py`. Hooks and formats may come from any niche. Comment, quote, and reply targets stay inside the owner's space. Every row cites the post link and its counts. A row under the bar can stay as a reader note.
