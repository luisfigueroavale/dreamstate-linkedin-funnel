# Stage 1

## Stage 1

1. Take the picked idea in their words. Use one pillar and one job. Ground it in `brand.local/` and `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`.

2. For stance, read what they already published on this. The recording extends it, or it names the change.

3. For facts, verify product claims, model claims, and market claims where they were published, and write the date beside each one. `write-research-brief` does that read. Do not invent the citation.

4. For the worked example and the resource, prefer what they can show, and a file that already exists. A new resource is `make-lead-magnet`, and only if they ask.

5. Packaging before the outline. `write-video-titles` writes two titles and two thumbnail directions. A structure counts as checked outside the niche only when `choose-next-video` says so. Otherwise it stays a hypothesis. The outline then has to make that promise true.

6. For the outline, `write-video-script` writes the opening minute word for word (that skill's gate on the first minute), then chapters. Each chapter says what is on screen and which claim it makes true. One chapter answers one objection a buyer raises, so a later reply can point at it. Use `write-video-script` for a full script when that helps.

7. Write the brief from `vault/week/brief.md`.

Ask only the release day, and their verdict when the recording is a review. Decide the rest and list it under Still open.
