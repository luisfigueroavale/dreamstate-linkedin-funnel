## Stage 2

Read `vault/board.md` and the brief for dates around the release, and for the same topic. Reuse a row that is already there.

This desk keeps one board, `vault/board.md`. Dreamstate holds the drafts. A second planning database would split status, so it is not used. Put the dated plan on the brief, and say it in chat.

`update-content-board` moves a line when status changes. `social.content.plan` sets a day mark on an existing draft and does not publish. That mark is not a schedule.

Default rows are the day table in `docs/plan-content-week.md`. Add a micro post, a chapter thread, or a repost of a winner only when they pick it. Keep the week near eight rows, not thirty. Other X posts are planned with them in `plan-x-week`.
