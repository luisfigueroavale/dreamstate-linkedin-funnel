# Stage 4

## Stage 4

| # | Step | Skill | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cut | `trim-talking-head-takes`, or `edit-long-video` when they want a designed open | False starts, empty gaps, and the leftover end of a screen demo are gone, and that skill's check passed |
| 2 | Transcript | The transcript they supply. `cut-raw-footage` can read it | Chapter times come from the finished cut, not the raw file |
| 3 | Titles | `write-video-titles` | Both titles are still true of the cut |
| 4 | Description | `write-video-description` | A block they can paste, with chapters |
| 5 | Thumbnail | `design-video-thumbnail` | Both sizes that skill names |
| 6 | Hand off | `publish-youtube-video` | That skill publishes after a yes on that exact video |

`publish-youtube-video` publishes on the connected YouTube account. Packaging is not that yes. Privacy stays private until they name otherwise. Do not copy a connection from another project. This desk does not ship a transcription service.
