# Stage 5

## Stage 5

Write the letter first with `write-newsletter-issue`. It is the argument the other pieces reuse. Then `write-x-article` from that letter, then `write-x-post` for the short note that the recording is up, then the LinkedIn posts.

LinkedIn copy goes through `write-linkedin-post` and `write-opening-lines`. On LinkedIn the URL goes in the comment under the post, and the post tells the reader to look there. A LinkedIn article is optional, and only if they ask: `adapt-essay-for-linkedin`.

Root 3 is the harvest. Read the finished-cut transcript and tag each idea, in this order: take, line, receipt, strong claim, proof moment, framework, chapter. Ask whether it can stand without the recording. If yes, use one chapter as its own post, a picture (`design-post-graphic` or `draw-process-map`), a proof, an opinion, or a short X post planned in `plan-x-week`.

If no, put it on Backlog. `split-long-source` helps the split. Pictures are HTML.
