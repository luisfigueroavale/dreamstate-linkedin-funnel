## Stage 8

When a piece goes live, set its status to Published on the board and on the brief. Rank what shipped this week and anything older that is still in market. Keep the best one or two as candidates to post again on the other platform, and as input to the next recording.

A customer win enters the next brief only with permission, and only with a number already cleared. Bring the winners and the sharpest questions when you ask what they want to make next. They still choose it.

On the brief, write the public links, any reply the tools returned, any signup they can show, how long the week took, which rows won, and what they chose to make next. Do not open a second log.

The close calls are in `docs/plan-content-week.md`. Fetch each id before you run it. Re-read the artifact. Do not guess a version and retry.
