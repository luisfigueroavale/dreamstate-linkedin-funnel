# Audit

Review only what was supplied. If there is no cut, audit the plan and say so.

Each finding: priority (blocker, major, minor), timestamp or line, the problem, what the viewer loses, the exact repair.

Score each area 0 to 5, then sum weight times rating over 5:

| Area | Weight |
|---|---|
| First five seconds and first frame | 20 |
| Clear outcome in plain words | 15 |
| Real proof and claim support | 25 |
| Muted clarity and picture matching words | 15 |
| Pacing and reading time | 10 |
| Difference and objection | 5 |
| One action that matches the destination | 5 |
| Export checked | 5 |

85 or more: ready for a real test. 70 to 84: targeted fixes. Under 70: rework. A blocker overrides the total. Areas not reviewed are marked, never counted as a pass.

Speed pass: once the base cut is clear, try about 1.10 times speed (60 seconds becomes about 54.5). Keep it only if words, sync, and output stay readable. Never use the faster cut as a product speed claim.

Finish with ready for testing, needs revision, or blocked by the named missing item.
