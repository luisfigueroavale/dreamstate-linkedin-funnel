# Audit

Copy `references/templates/audit.md`. Review only what was supplied. If there is no cut, audit the plan and say so.

Each finding: priority (blocker, major, minor), the timestamp or line, the problem, what the viewer loses, and the exact repair.

Score each area 0 to 5, then sum weight times rating over 5:

| Area | Weight |
|---|---|
| First five seconds and first frame | 20 |
| Clear outcome in plain words | 15 |
| Real proof, every claim on a ledger line | 25 |
| Clear with the sound off, picture matches words | 15 |
| Pacing and reading time | 10 |
| Difference and objection | 5 |
| One action that matches the destination | 5 |
| Export checked with `check-short-export` | 5 |

85 or more: ready for a real test. 70 to 84: targeted fixes. Under 70: rework. A blocker overrides the total. An area not reviewed is marked, never counted as a pass.

Blockers: a claim with no `verified` ledger line, an unlabeled concept or demo screen, a spliced fake run, a generated face or body, private data on screen, a competitor named, an em dash or a banned word in the copy, or paid audio with no yes on file.

Speed pass: once the base cut is clear, try about 1.10 times speed (60 seconds becomes about 54.5). Keep it only if words, sync, and output stay readable. Never use the faster cut as a product speed claim.

Finish with one verdict: ready for testing, needs revision, or blocked by the named missing item.
