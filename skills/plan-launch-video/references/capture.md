# Capture list

Per shot: starting state, input, exact actions, output, capture range with handles, crop, cursor path, and the ledger line it proves. Clean demo account, realistic data, nothing private on screen.

Label every shot as real time, sped up, waiting cut, illustration, or mockup. Never splice separate runs into one fake continuous run.

Keep the picture moving with purpose: a push, a crop to the control, a pan to the output. No flat screenshot held for ten seconds. Give the output enough time to read at phone size.

Captions, emphasis text, and real UI text must agree and not cover each other. Hand rendering to `make-launch-clip` and captions to `render-captioned-video`.
