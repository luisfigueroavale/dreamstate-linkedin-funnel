# Market

- Name the exact viewer in a real moment, not a broad role.
- List what they love, what they wish existed, and what they hate. Look for the promise where those meet.
- Collect their own phrases from sources the owner supplied or that you read this session, with link and date. Never invent a quote.
- Name the argument already live in that audience and the product's honest answer to it.
- Pick one concrete grievance over a general complaint.

Positioning, internal only:

```text
For <viewer> who wants <result>, <product> turns <input> into <visible output> through <observable difference>. Unlike <alternative>, it <defensible distinction>.
```

Rank angles by how much the viewer wants it, how strong the proof is, how clear it is at once, and whether it can be filmed. Go from feature to mechanism to visible result to wanted outcome without skipping a step the proof does not cover.
