# Proof ledger

An aha moment is the short visible event where a skeptical viewer sees something useful happen. A click is an action. A usable result appearing is the payoff.

For each candidate: problem, input, action, output, why it matters, how it differs, the evidence file, whether it can be captured, and its limits. Rank by relevance, clarity at a glance, surprise, credibility, and effort.

Each claim gets a ledger line: wording, source, date, scope, limits, the shot that shows it, and status (verified, supplied but unchecked, unsupported, remove). Funding proves funding, not product quality. A superlative needs a defined category and a real comparison.

Proof unit: input the viewer recognizes, real product action, output they can inspect, why it matters.

If `skills/study-launch-videos` reports the library is pending, say so in the plan and continue without it.
