# Proof ledger

An aha moment is the short, visible event where a skeptical viewer sees something useful happen. A click is an action. A usable result appearing on screen is the payoff.

Copy `references/templates/proof-ledger.md`. For each aha moment, write the problem, the input, the action, the output, why it matters, how it differs, the evidence file, whether it can be captured, and its limits. Rank by relevance to the viewer, clarity at a glance, surprise, credibility, and effort to capture.

Each claim gets one ledger line: the exact wording, the feature or source it rests on, the date, the scope, the limits, the shot that shows it, and a status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| verified | A shot of the real product shows it, or a named source supports the exact wording and scope. Read this session. A product page, a press mention, or funding alone never verifies a claim |
| supplied | The owner supplied it and it is not checked yet |
| unsupported | No source. Cut it or ask for one |
| remove | Wrong, private, or not allowed. It stays out |

Only `verified` lines may be spoken, captioned, or posted. Funding, a raise, or investor names prove funding, not the product. A superlative needs a defined category and a real comparison, so in practice it stays out. A customer name, a logo, or a quote needs written permission on file.

Proof unit: an input the viewer recognizes, a real product action, an output they can inspect, and why it matters.

Comparables: run `study-launch-videos` with the brief's product type, proof type, and length. When it returns `launch video library pending` or `no comparable launches in the library`, say so in the plan and continue without it.
