# Timeline

Starting points, not platform rules. Times in seconds.

| Length | Beats |
|---|---|
| 15 | 0 to 2 hook; 2 to 10 one proof unit; 10 to 12 result; 12 to 15 one action |
| 30 | 0 to 3 strongest result; 3 to 5 promise; 5 to 21 demo; 21 to 25 objection or authority if needed; 25 to 30 action |
| 60 | 0 to 5 hook; 5 to 15 first aha; 15 to 30 second aha; 30 to 43 use case; 43 to 51 objection or authority; 51 to 60 action |
| 90 | 0 to 5 hook; 5 to 30 main proof; 30 to 55 deeper demos; 55 to 72 difference or objection; 72 to 80 authority if useful; 80 to 90 action |
| 120 | 0 to 5 hook; 5 to 30 strongest proof; 30 to 70 linked demos; 70 to 95 deeper proof; 95 to 108 objection or authority; 108 to 120 action |

Clear value by second five. Real demo starting by second ten at the latest. End when the claim is proven.

Each beat row: start, end, one idea, the spoken line, the visible action, on-screen words, motion, sound cue, the ledger line, and what the viewer should conclude. Plan at 130 to 160 spoken words a minute and label it an estimate.

Use one company, dataset, or project across beats so they build on each other. One action at the end, matching the post.
