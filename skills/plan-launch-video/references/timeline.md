# Timed structures

Starting points, not platform rules. Times are in seconds. Pick the structure from the brief's `Length (seconds)`. For a length between rows, use the nearest row, stretch or trim its beats to fit, and say so in the plan. The channel sets the frame and the pace, not the length. Finding comparables uses the library's own wider bands (see `study-launch-videos`), not this table. The library's `data/structures.json` keeps its own reference beat maps, which differ in places. This table is the planning source, and a plan never edits that file.

| Length | Beats |
|---|---|
| 15 | 0 to 2 hook; 2 to 10 one proof unit; 10 to 13 result; 13 to 15 one action |
| 30 | 0 to 3 strongest result; 3 to 5 promise; 5 to 22 demo; 22 to 26 objection or limit, if needed; 26 to 30 action |
| 45 | 0 to 4 hook; 4 to 18 first aha; 18 to 32 second aha; 32 to 39 control shot or objection; 39 to 45 action |
| 60 | 0 to 5 hook; 5 to 15 first aha; 15 to 30 second aha; 30 to 43 use case; 43 to 51 objection or control shot; 51 to 60 action |
| 90 | 0 to 5 hook; 5 to 30 main proof; 30 to 55 deeper demos; 55 to 72 difference or objection; 72 to 80 founder fact, if useful; 80 to 90 action |
| 120 | 0 to 5 hook; 5 to 30 strongest proof; 30 to 70 linked demos; 70 to 95 deeper proof; 95 to 108 objection or control shot; 108 to 120 action |

Value is clear by second five. A real demo starts by second ten at the latest. End when the claim is proven.

Copy `references/templates/script-storyboard.md`. Each beat row has start, end, one idea, the spoken line, the visible action, on-screen words, motion, sound cue, the ledger line, and what the viewer should conclude. Plan at 130 to 160 spoken words a minute, and label the word count an estimate.

Use one company, dataset, or project across the beats so each beat builds on the last. One action at the end, and it matches the post.
