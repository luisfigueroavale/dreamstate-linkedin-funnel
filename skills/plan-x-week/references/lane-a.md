# Lane A

Lane A is an adapted LinkedIn piece. Open a recent LinkedIn post in the vault or on the account.

Keep it when one experience is doing the work: a result they can point at, a mistake that left a rule, or a choice they made. Carry the point. The LinkedIn shape stays on LinkedIn.

You may keep their opening words exactly, then move to the point at once. If they replace that opening, the draft uses their line unchanged.

Put the X version on the day after the LinkedIn day. They can swap those days.

Name a product or a person only with a handle already in their notes, a paste, or a tool result from this session. If the handle is missing, ask. Do not guess one.

A comment-to-DM resource often does worse here than on LinkedIn. Measure their own posts before you plan another. If they still want the file on X, the post stands on its own.

Do not carry counts over from LinkedIn, and do not write "now on X too". The picture keeps the two-step ask: the first step says follow, the second says comment the keyword.

Do not crop that box off, and do not leave a connect line on the X picture. A new frame goes through `design-post-graphic`, with both steps still on it.

The keyword is the one `send-lead-magnet` will use. This skill does not arm the rule. A send waits for yes on that rule.
