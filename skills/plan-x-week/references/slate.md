# Slate

Otherwise slate about 1.5 times the slots. If they have not said how many X posts a day, ask once and wait. Do not pick a daily count for them. Use one table per day:

| Day | Lane | Angle | First line | Asset | Source |

The first line is the full opening and already follows the writing rules. Angle, asset, and source are one line each. Asset is the file they will attach, or none. Source is a path or url you can open.

Mark one row per day with `suggest`. They pick, cut, and edit. Keep their words. An empty lane stays empty. Do not invent a row to fill the table. The slate is not a post.
