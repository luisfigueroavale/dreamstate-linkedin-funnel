# Verdicts

Append to `vault/week/<date>-x-verdicts.md` after the session. Use the brief's date when the brief exists, otherwise the session date, as YYYY-MM-DD. Create the file with this header and no sample rows:

| Date | Piece | Verdict | Why |

Put the newest row at the bottom. Write one row per keep, cut, or rewrite. The verdict is kept, cut, or rewritten, in their words when they gave them.

The why is the rule for the next slate. The next slate starts from these rows.
