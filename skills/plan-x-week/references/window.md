# Window

`plan-content-week` owns the video post, including the short note that the video is up, and the X essay. Other X posts come from here: a short post, or a thread when the steps are required, including a chapter thread. Replies on someone else's post are `draft-x-replies`.

The window is the next 7 days unless they name another. Before the slate, read every `vault/week/*-x-verdicts.md`, oldest first.

Then read what is already planned on every channel in that window: the week brief, `vault/board.md`, `vault/posts/`, and `social.content.artifact_list` (free read).

Search the vault with `search-vault` before you treat a prior post as theirs. Leave out the video post, the X essay, and anything already planned, so the same piece is not set twice.
