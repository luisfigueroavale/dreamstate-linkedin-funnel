# Writing

When writing, put one point in the first line. The post stays short. Voice is `brand.local/voice.md` and X posts they have already published.

Read `vault/notes/pillars.md`, `vault/notes/concepts.md`, and `vault/notes/hooks.md` before the draft. An empty note is named, not filled.

Use a thread only when the steps are required.

In a thread, each post still makes its point if the reader stops. The post has to stand alone. If it needs the LinkedIn piece or the video beside it, fix it or cut it.

Against slop, allow no role-verbs that only sound busy, no label-colon bullets, and no bonus tag. Bullets are fine. A stack names each tool and the job it does. A process is first person and uses plain verbs.

When posts from people they follow are already in hand, check the draft against that rule. If those posts are not in hand, say so.

Keep the tool, the number, and the mistake that make it theirs. Cut the rest. Use numerals.

A video link is the full https URL. A link to their own site uses `utm_source=x` and the campaign slug on the week brief, including when the piece was adapted from LinkedIn.

Links to other sites stay clean.
