Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

After a post is live, keep the post id you will harvest. When that post is a stored artifact, read the delivery back with `social.content.artifact_get`. Take `linkedin_account_id` from `senders.linkedin_accounts.list`. If that list has more than one account, ask which one published and wait. `visibility.linkedin.engagement_harvest` requires `worksheet_id`, `linkedin_account_id` from `senders.linkedin_accounts.list`, and `post_id`. Dry run is allowed and costs 0. Live needs yes. Then `visibility.linkedin.engagement_status` and `visibility.linkedin.engagement_engagers`. This is not `tables.profile_watcher` and not `tables.x_post_engagers.run`. Collect reactions and commenters for that one post into the worksheet you named. The harvest messages nobody and does not edit the post. Run it on posts you published, inside a window you name. Do not walk every older post because a column is blank. Results stop at the provider's page size, at the data it actually has, and at the credit cap. A short page is not proof the post had no response. Keep that person's profile and the post they touched on the same row. Do not move one employee's reaction onto a colleague at the same company. When a current employer is present, leave it on that person. When it is absent, employer stays unknown. A reaction is only something observed. It is not evidence they intend to buy. `knowledge.engagement.people_engaged_n_times` counts mail and meetings. It is not this list.

`tables.profile_watcher.preview` is free, max 10 person URLs, no network. Use real public person URLs. Do not submit an example. It returns `preview_hash`. It saves nothing, pulls no one, and schedules nothing. Before the yes, read the listed profiles, the cadence, how far back the preview says it looks, the coverage note, the issues, and the estimated credits. The stored watcher is daily. That cap is per later run. It is not a monthly budget and it is not a forecast of the price. `tables.profile_watcher.create` needs `preview_hash` and `approved: true` and owner yes because later runs spend. The create call does not write engager rows. It is not the harvest. Start with the owner's own public profile. Add another URL only when that audience is already on the buyer page. A new URL or a new cap means a new preview. `tables.profile_watcher.resume` takes that preview and the same yes. If create times out, read the run with `runs_get` and reuse the same `idempotency_key`. Do not open a second watcher. Do not add another collector that fills a second table for those same profiles. Stopping or replacing a URL leaves the collected rows where they are. Read coverage and credits already spent before the list grows. The watcher does not score, enroll, hand over a file, or message anyone. Sending stays a separate yes.

Score only after the worksheet columns and both rubrics are in front of you. If Ideal Customer is empty, stop and run `context-intake`. If `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` is missing or still says `Status: blank`, stop. Do not invent a market. The company rubric comes from that buyer page and from exclusions you have actually named. The person rubric is the filled follower file, including the qualifying test the owner marked. Add two `column.ai_fit_score` columns with `tables.columns.add`, company and person. Each returns one score from 0 to 100. The add is free and does not execute. If the columns already exist, reuse them. Do not add a second pair. If the rubric must change, `tools_search` then `tools_get` the update capability. Do not guess its id. Bind only keys that sheet really has. The score reads those cells. It does not start a web search to fill a hole, and it must not invent one. A later lookup, if you still need it, is `visibility.research.web_search` (2 credits) or `visibility.research.urls_fetch` (1). Both refuse dry_run. Quote the price, wait, then live. Do not send dry_run. Do not swap in some other provider. Connected-account calls stay inside that account's limits. Score company and person apart. A required unknown on the qualifying test is a hold, not a no and not a yes. Hold is not enrolled and is not sent. That block is the warm note and the sequence. The file they already asked for is still `magnet-send`. A company fail stops even when the person test is yes. A required no with evidence on the row fails that side. A no without evidence holds. A question the owner did not mark required can sit in the note. It does not fail the row by itself. A like, a comment, or a keyword does not answer the test. Missing headcount, remit, or intent stays unknown. A company name alone is not a domain and not an identity match. Exclude your own team, current customers, and open threads with a column condition when the worksheet has those fields. If it does not, say the exclusion could not be applied. Do not delete the rows to hide them.

Dry-run `tables.columns.run` on one row you can judge. Show the prompt that will be sent, the bound cells, the credits, and `quote_hash`. A dry run prices that preparation. It does not prove the judgment. Then live, `max_rows` inside params, on a handful: someone who fits, a company that fails, a person who fails, and a row whose required field is empty. Poll that run with `runs_get`, then read the reasons. If they would fail `npm run fit`, rewrite the rubric and dry-run again. Do not widen the audience to hide a bad rubric. The pass line is the owner's, written on the Ideal Customer page. A borrowed 70 is not the default. Both scores have to clear it, and every required question needs evidence. People who already passed may be ordered by how recent the engagement was. Do not add an engagement bonus that turns a fail into a pass. A pass is not a yes to send.

`tables.auto_run.set` is a standing spend that waits for yes. After that yes, set `approved: true`, because it arms a recurring run. It runs the named score columns when rows arrive, inside `max_credits_per_run`. Read what those columns depend on before you arm it. It is separate from the harvest and from the watcher. Batches you can read are enough until the sample holds. A required field that is still empty stays a hold, and the row can be scored again after the evidence changes. Editing the rubric leaves stored cells as they are. Read them before a forced rerun. One pair of columns on this worksheet is enough. Do not publish a shared routine you have not fetched.

A resource ask goes to `magnet-send` first. Check that the file URL still matches the promise, then hand the ask over. Do not block the file on a score. Do not say you were watching. They asked, so answer the ask. A warm note only after both pass, capped, no pitch, owner yes per item. Use the topic in words that post already used. One question. No offer and no calendar link unless they already asked to meet. Anyone with an open thread stays out unless the owner names those rows. Capture, scoring, the file, and enrollment do not perform each other. A standing outreach yes must name the eligible cohort, the destination sequence, the limits, and the stops. Engagement alone is not fit and is not permission to widen who gets a message. This skill does not call `sequence.sequences.activate_audience`.

Comments on your post are `social.comments.queue_list`. Before an empty queue is believed, refresh with `visibility.linkedin.comments.refresh` (`delivery_id`). Articles and newsletters return 422. Follow the cursor. Do not hammer the same page. `visibility.linkedin_post_notifications_list` takes its `delivery_id` from `social.content.article_deliveries_list`, and may take `limit` up to 100. It has no next page. A busy post stops at 100. Preview with `social.comments.reply`. `social.comments.approve` posts that preview and needs `action_id`, a 64-hex `content_hash`, and `approved: true`, only after yes on that text. Do not mint an approval. If the effect is unknown, read the run. Do not send it again as a guess.

`visibility.analytics_query` reads a stored snapshot and does not refresh it. Read coverage and freshness beside the totals. Null means unavailable. Do not put zero where the tool said nothing. Reach is not a pipeline. When that snapshot says a refresh is required, `visibility.analytics_refresh` needs `platform`, `account_id` from `senders.social_accounts.list`, and `metric_scope` of `account`, `post`, or `audience`. Post scope also needs `provider_post_id`. It has no dry_run. Use smoke_test. Read `freshness.state`. `unsupported` means nothing was fetched. Carry the source id, the post id, and the event id through the score and through any later sequence result. A repeating monitor is `workflow.workflows.activate`, and it waits for yes when it will spend or send. Report published state, the resource URL, and captured, qualified, and held counts when a tool returned them, plus the object links. What was not returned stays unknown.
