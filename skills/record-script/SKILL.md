---
name: record-script
description: "Write a script the owner can record, with a viewer promise, sourced beats, and a payoff that the footage will actually show. Use before filming. It does not generate footage."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Open this only after `video-pick` has passed its three gates. If gate 3 already scripted the opening minute, keep it unless the owner rewrites it. You are writing words and a shot list. A script is not a rendered video. It is not a published video. Do not generate footage.

Read the source they named, all the way through. When that source is an article, read the article. Walk the real workflow the way it runs today. Read the voice in `brand.local/voice.md`, and any sample that file names. The viewer is the reader in `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`. A missing file, or `Status: blank`, means stop and ask.

Name the end state: what they will know, and what they can do once the video stops. Then confirm the form they will work from. The form is one of three. A read-aloud script. An outline. A shot list.

The order stays the same in every form.

1. Promise. Say that end state in the opening.
2. Enough context. Only what they need before the next step.
3. Ordered demonstration. Where the piece teaches without a click, use a plain explanation, in the order the workflow runs.
4. Limits. What this will not do, and where it fails.
5. Result. The payoff the recording can show.
6. Next action. One move that follows from that result.

`title-pair` writes titles. If a title is already chosen, the promise is that title's claim.

Each beat carries spoken copy, then the visual, then the source. Spoken copy uses sentences, and a transition that names the next picture. The visual is the screen, the gesture, or the object in frame. The source is the file, the page, or the workflow step you walked. Cut a beat that has no source.

Prerequisites sit before the step that needs them. So does the setup friction: a login, a sample file, a permission, a first-run dialog. That friction is its own beat.

Show the real output when it exists. Name the file or the screen you opened. Label a proposed screen as proposed. A placeholder gets that same label. A feature that does not work is never described as shown. A step you could not run stays unshown.

Keep the length proportionate to the runtime they requested. Read every spoken line aloud. Cut a line you cannot finish in one breath. Time that read, then trim or restore beats until it fits. Do not add lines to fill the clock.

Mark on-screen beats in brackets.

The form they confirmed is the one written to be used. Still return the script, the shot list, and the missing assets. The shot list repeats the order: bracket, visual, source, and whether the asset is in hand. A missing asset is a screen, a file, a number, or a clip the words name and you do not have. Leave it on that list. Speak a number only when the source or the walk returned it.

`video-blurb` starts once a transcript of the final cut is in hand. Chapters wait for that transcript. `thumb-frame` comes after the title. The picture has to be a promise the video can show.

Run `brand/taste.md` before the owner sees the pages. Say that nothing was recorded.
