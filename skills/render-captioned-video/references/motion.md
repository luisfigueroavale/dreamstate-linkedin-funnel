## Motion and sound

Scan one frame every 10 seconds and label that frame with its timestamp. Sixteen frames fill one sheet. Then read positions from frames that carry a 10 percent grid.

An interlude is a full-frame flat card with centred type for the one idea or number the piece hangs on. Cut that card in hard. It lasts 1 to 4 seconds. Past 4 seconds the cut looks paused. Under 1 second the next card has not landed.

Reframes and zooms are hard cuts. On shorts and vertical cuts only, keep the scale between 1.1 and 1.3, about every 2 to 3 seconds.

Soft motion is only for a type entrance: the words rise out of a blur, with no spring and no bounce.

At most one sound effect per hit. A hit is one visual event that earns a sound. Do not stack two effects on the same appearance. Space hits at least 1.5 seconds apart. The checker warns when they sit closer. This script does not mix the effect.

It checks the path and the gap.

## Card limits

One full-frame idea card in any 30 seconds. One chapter card in any 45 seconds. A phrase card is one to six spoken words, not a sentence. A list card is three to six items they said. Do not repeat a label the picture already shows large.

Break the pattern about every 30 seconds.

On a vertical frame, keep cards out of the top 10 percent and the bottom 22 percent. A full-frame idea card cuts in hard. A spotlight label must not cover the evidence. Cut a graphic that only repeats the screen, including a card that only echoes the captions.

Record what the owner kept or changed so the next edit starts from that note.
