# Report

State the evidence label. Use source only, prompt only, rendered, or rendered and inspected.

In this list, rendered means the file exists and you have not looked. A clean render does not mean every slide was seen.

Open on the verdict and on what you could not inspect. Give the few findings that most change what the reader takes away.

Each one names the place, what the reader does because of it, and the correction to make.

Keep three piles: broken behavior, a clash with a rule they adopted, and a preference. Change hierarchy, type, spacing, or crop before you ask for extra decoration.

A readable difference they made on purpose is not a defect.

This pass is report-only unless they asked for fixes. A fix stays on this asset, keeps edits already in the file, then renders again.

Reinspect that export before you call the item resolved. Start from the largest fault. Two or three passes.

Reviewing one frame does not rewrite brand files. It does not publish.
