# Who is asking

Decide who is asking. Match a whole-word keyword, plus the typo list. A real request also includes send or share plus the file's noun, a bare "please send", a line that only asks you to send it, or a genuine ask such as wanting the kit. Skip praise and your own comments.

Collapse one person's comments into one decision. SECOND_DEGREE, DISTANCE_2, THIRD_DEGREE, and DISTANCE_3 get the connect-first line only. FIRST_DEGREE or DISTANCE_1 gets the file. A missing degree is a skip. Accepting an invite is a separate yes, and it is not this delivery.

A quota refusal parks the run until the quota returns and can stall later file messages for hours, even on an optional read. Those reads share a small daily budget. Open one only while someone waits and budget remains. Accept an invite only from a person waiting on this file.
