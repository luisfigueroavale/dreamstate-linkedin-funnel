## Reader

Read `audience/AGENTS.md`. Use `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` as the reader. The buyer is the Ideal Customer page in Company Context, not this file. If the local file is missing or `Status: blank`, stop and ask. Do not guess who the reader is.

## First

Read `brand/AGENTS.md` and follow its read order. Drafting needs `brand.local/identity.md` and `brand.local/voice.md` filled. Missing, empty, or `Status: blank` means stop and ask the owner. Do not guess identity, voice, or proof.

## Last

Run `brand/taste.md` on the result. Fix a miss you can fix without inventing a fact. Name any miss that needs the owner. Only then hand it over.

# Write the brief

Read `docs/sources.md`. Open the files or pages the owner named. For a page on the public web, `tools_get` `visibility.research.urls_fetch` or `visibility.research.web_search` and treat them as paid until the schema says otherwise. Dry run, show the price, wait.

Record for each source: what it is, the date you read it, and the line you are willing to use. Inferences are labeled. Gaps go under Open. The brief is `vault/sources/` only if the owner wants it local and gitignored. Otherwise leave it in the chat and in the week brief.

Do not turn a thin source into a number.

## Done

Sources used, claims you will allow, and claims you cut.
