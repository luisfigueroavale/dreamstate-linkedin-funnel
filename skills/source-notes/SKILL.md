---
name: source-notes
description: "Build an evidence brief for a content or go-to-market question from primary sources, with implications and open questions. Use before a claim-heavy draft. It does not invent findings."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Write question, audience, time window, and decision first. Post audience: filled `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`. Buying: Ideal Customer. Keep them apart. A blank reader file stops the brief.

Before a paid call, cheapest first: `vault/sources/`, a named page, `vault/voice/`, `vault/notes/`, then an in-session Company Context page (id and revision). URL only: fetch it. Do not search to relocate it.

`whoami` before first spend. `tools_get` before `tools_run`. Call prices and modes: `docs/calls.md`. `approved: true` only if the contract requires it, after yes. A refused live call stops you.

`visibility.research.web_search` when local files leave the question open. Required `query`. Optional `max_results`, `include_domains`. Do not guess a host.

`visibility.research.urls_fetch` for a known URL. Required `urls`. Optional `max_characters`. A cut-off tail is partial, not empty.

A company claim needs that company's publication plus a second publisher when a sentence depends on it. Write publication date and observation date, or that publication date is missing. Outside the time window is not the present.

`source.account_posts_linkedin_profile` and `source.linkedin_posts_search` are definition ids, not `tools_run` ids. `tools_get` is not found for both. Do not attach or run a feed. See `docs/sources.md`.

A suggestion or a promo line is not an owner fact. Prefer how the work runs and a measured result. Say what each title supports. Lead with the conclusion, the strongest evidence, and the meaning for that reader. Confidence names its limit.

Row: claim, path (URL or file), excerpt or paraphrase, date, confidence, use. Keep evidence, inference, conflict, and open questions apart. Cite a vault file, a named page, or this call's figure, with the capability id.

Write `vault/sources/<date>-brief.md`. If taken, add a slug. `vault/sources/README.md`: path, day, research brief. Every gap goes under Open. Do not zero a missing number.

Owner facts: `context-intake` only. An owner-only gap: one blank question under Open in `vault/intake/interview.md`, and blank here too.
