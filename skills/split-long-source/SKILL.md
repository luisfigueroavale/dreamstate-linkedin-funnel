# Split a long source

Split one long source into separate pieces and picture briefs that keep its facts and its voice. Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when

- The source is an interview, a newsletter, a talk, or any other long source.

- It does not add a claim, post, or schedule.

## Steps

1. Read the whole source before a cut, and if it is not already in `vault/sources/`, preserve it with `collect-owner-context` first (`references/source.md`).

2. Read `vault/notes/pillars.md` and the offer in `brand.local/identity.md` (`references/source.md`).

3. Record a section or a timestamp on each candidate (`references/candidates.md`).

4. Rank by the reader in `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`, the pillars, and the offer (`references/candidates.md`).

5. Give each piece one point, a source section, a pillar, a funnel job, and a format (`references/piece.md`).

6. Pick the shape before the sentences (`references/shapes.md`).

7. Match the CTA to the current offer and that piece's funnel job (`references/handoff.md`).

8. Write the source-to-piece map outside the copy (`references/handoff.md`).

9. Save a draft with `social.content.artifact_create` when they ask to store a piece, and that call saves the draft and does not post (`references/handoff.md`).

## Stop rules

- Follow the skill-frame writing rules.

- Add no claim the source does not already make.

- A disagreement the source left open stays open.

- An empty pillar list means ask.

- Rewording is not a second piece.

- Do not turn a guest's experience into the owner's first person.

- Voice stays the owner's.

- A number that appears twice must match.

- Skip a shape the source cannot carry.

- You may not claim a render you did not inspect.

- Do not open every piece by announcing it was split.

- Stop at the count they asked for.

- Do not write a second database.

- Do not post or schedule.
