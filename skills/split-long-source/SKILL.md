---
name: split-long-source
description: "Split one long source into separate pieces and picture briefs that keep its facts and its voice. Use for an interview, a newsletter, a talk, or any other long source. It does not add a claim, post, or schedule."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Take one source, cut it into separate pieces, and keep the same facts. Read the whole source before a cut. If it is not already in `vault/sources/`, preserve it with `collect-owner-context` first. Read `vault/notes/pillars.md` and the offer in `brand.local/identity.md`. On each candidate, record a section or a timestamp. Pull ideas, examples, disagreements, processes, and evidence that can each stand alone. A disagreement the source left open stays open. Rank by usefulness to the reader in `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`, and by fit with the pillars and the offer, not by how many posts you can mint.

Each piece has one point, a source section, a pillar, a funnel job, and a format. The pillar is a name already in `vault/notes/pillars.md`. The funnel job comes from `vault/notes/funnel-stages.md`. An empty pillar list means ask. Rewording is not a second piece. Keep the uncertainty, the time period, and the scope on that point. Do not turn a guest's experience into the owner's first person. Voice stays the owner's. A number that appears twice must match. Add no claim the source does not already make.

Pick the shape before the sentences. LinkedIn (a fold, then an ask) goes to `write-linkedin-post`. X (one claim) goes to `write-x-post`. A letter (one takeaway) goes to `write-newsletter-issue`. A spoken script (the payoff is on screen) goes to `write-video-script`. A native article (the argument, no new proof) goes to `write-long-essay`. Skip a shape the source cannot carry. When a relationship is clearer as a picture, a process goes to `draw-process-map`, and a still, a portrait, or a slide goes to `design-post-graphic`. A video brief can be a storyboard and a shot list. Each shot names its section or timestamp. You may not claim a render you did not inspect.

The CTA matches the current offer in `brand.local/identity.md` and that piece's funnel job. When attribution helps the reader, name the original source in the piece. Do not open every piece by announcing it was split. Stop at the count they asked for. If the source supports fewer, deliver that smaller count and explain the shortfall. Outside the copy, write the source-to-piece map: one line per piece, with the point, the section or timestamp, the pillar, the funnel job, and the format. On a week, add that map to `vault/week/<date>.md`. When they ask to store a piece, `social.content.artifact_create` saves that draft and does not post. Do not write a second database. Do not post or schedule.
