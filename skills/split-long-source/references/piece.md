# Piece

Each piece has one point, a source section, a pillar, a funnel job, and a format. The pillar is a name already in `vault/notes/pillars.md`.

The funnel job comes from `vault/notes/funnel-stages.md`. An empty pillar list means ask. Rewording is not a second piece.

Keep the uncertainty, the time period, and the scope on that point. Do not turn a guest's experience into the owner's first person. Voice stays the owner's.

A number that appears twice must match. Add no claim the source does not already make.
