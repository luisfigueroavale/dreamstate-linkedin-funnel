# Shapes

Pick the shape before the sentences.

LinkedIn (a fold, then an ask) goes to `write-linkedin-post`. X (one claim) goes to `write-x-post`. A letter (one takeaway) goes to `write-newsletter-issue`.

A spoken script (the payoff is on screen) goes to `write-video-script`. A native article (the argument, no new proof) goes to `write-long-essay`.

When a relationship is clearer as a picture, a process goes to `draw-process-map`, and a still, a portrait, or a slide goes to `design-post-graphic`.

A video brief can be a storyboard and a shot list. Each shot names its section or timestamp.
