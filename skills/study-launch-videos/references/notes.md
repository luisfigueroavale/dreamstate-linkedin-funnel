# Notes

Per record, one block:

```text
<file name> (<post_url>)
Company: <company or unknown>. Product: <product or none>. Posted by: <posted_by>.
Category: <category>. Kind: <kind>. Length: <duration_seconds or unknown> s.
Hook family: <hook_family> (<hook_basis>). Kind label: <kind_basis>.
Beats: <start to end: idea, in your own words, one line each, from structure only, or none>
Numbers: views <value or unknown>, likes <value or unknown>, reposts <value or unknown>, replies <value or unknown>, bookmarks <value or unknown> (scope <scope>, retrieved <retrieved_on>, source <source>), one line per snapshot
Evidence: observed (observed-in-video, or an individual-post snapshot from a tool) or reported (inferred-from-post, or a listing-snapshot)
What to borrow: <one or two lines, in your own words>
```

`scene_cuts` are timestamps only and never show what happens in a shot, so never write a beat from them. Write patterns in your own words. Never paste narration, captions, or post text, and never copy the stored `excerpt`. It is source context only. A record's `analysis` is a study note, not a transcript. A reported number is reported, not verified reach. A company name may appear in this block for the owner. It never goes into a draft meant for posting.

End with the comparables block for the proof ledger, or the exact line from `references/library.md` when there is nothing to return.
