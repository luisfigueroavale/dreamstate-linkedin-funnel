# Concepts

On-image type is two to four words. A numeral on the image has to appear in the video. Mark any figure still waiting on proof.

Separate it by the setting, by the one mark, and by the hook words.

No generated face. Do not smooth a face they gave you, and do not swap it. A portrait stays the photo they supplied.

Colour in a concept comes from `brand.local`. If that file is blank or missing, stop and ask. Do not guess a colour.

Write four to six concepts. Each one names the words on the image, how the frame is arranged, which title it sits with, and any figure still waiting on proof.

Return the references from the set, what to borrow, what to avoid, and those concepts for `design-video-thumbnail`. The owner picks before a frame is built.
