# Defaults

Silence -45 dB. Minimum gap 600 ms. Pad 60 ms before and 150 ms after. 30 ms audio fades.

Picture encode CRF 18, H.264, preset `medium`, `yuv420p`. Audio AAC 192k, 48 kHz stereo. Check with `ffprobe` and `loudnorm`.

Trim on a word, not in the middle of one. Do not resample the aspect. Do not blend faces. `brand.local` is not required unless you add type. Do not upload.

Usually you want the best complete performance, and that is the last attempt that finishes. Remove starts they abandoned, long dead air, and setup or search transitions that appear on screen.

Keep short natural breaths. Keep the speaker's cadence. Keep the source resolution, the frame rate, the framing, and the colour.

No colour grading unless they ask. The same goes for captions, music, compression, zooms, reframing, overlays, and graphics. A designed opener is `edit-long-video`.
