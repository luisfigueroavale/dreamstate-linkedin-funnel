# Pieces

Choose angles the supplied material can support.

Mix pillars only among names already in `vault/notes/pillars.md`.

One piece for every pillar is optional.

Skip an idea already used on the board unless this piece contributes a point the earlier one lacks.

Each piece gets one pillar, one topic, one subtopic, and one funnel job named in `vault/notes/funnel-stages.md` (reach, belief, or signup).

If no pillar fits, leave the field empty.

When that note is still empty, name the gap and point at `map-content-pillars`.

Do not fill it here.

Do not mint a pillar, a topic, or a job to fill an empty day.

When material is missing, finish the pieces that have a source and list the gap.

Do not add posts just to reach a number.

The letter exists before posts that reuse it.

LinkedIn goes through `write-linkedin-post`, the letter through `write-newsletter-issue`, X through `write-x-post`.

Any other channel follows `brand.local/voice.md` and the format already written on the file.
