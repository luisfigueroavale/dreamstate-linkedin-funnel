name: video-pick
description: "Decide whether an idea is worth recording, using only evidence the owner can support. Use before record-script. It does not call a video-host API."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Three gates, then a ledger. No recording until the gates pass. The owner picks the idea. Gate 1: the idea sits deep in the niche, and you compare its ratio with the channel median they supplied. Gate 2: a structure counts as validated outside the niche only with at least 3× from 3 or more channels across 2 or more niches, in evidence they provided. Gate 3: the first minute is scripted. Ledger the day after publish, then three days out, a week out, and four weeks out: views per day against that channel median. Click-through and retention only when the owner pastes the studio numbers. Do not invent a video-host API.
