---
name: voice-card
description: "Derive voice rules from the owner's own samples and edits. Use when Tone of Voice or brand.local/voice.md is thin. It does not import another person's voice."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Only the owner's samples and the edits they later made. Write five before/after pairs into `brand.local/voice.md`: the line they handed you, the line they kept, and the single rule that edit implies. Add a banned-phrase list from words they struck, not from a generic style sheet. Fewer than five real edits means you write the ones you have and mark the rest open. Do not import another person's voice. Do not invent a sample to fill the five.
