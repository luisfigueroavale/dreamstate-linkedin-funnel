name: workspace-check
description: "Confirm the active Dreamstate workspace before a write or a spend. When the owner names person profiles, preview them and stop before a watcher exists. Use at the start of a later session. It does not draft or send."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

`whoami` first. Tell the owner which workspace is active, in chat only. Do not write the name or any id into the repo. If another workspace is active, stop. When `whoami` lists more than one workspace, ask the owner to name one and wait. Stop if any later call switches workspace. If `whoami` says this workspace cannot be used, repeat the guidance it returned and stop before you propose a watcher, a column, or a send. If `whoami` fails, stop. Do not register a second server. Dreamstate keeps the rows, the schedule, and delivery.

Three free reads come next. `senders.linkedin_accounts.list` (optional `limit`). `social.channels_list` (optional `limit`). `senders.social_accounts.list` (optional `platform`, `limit`) is where draft account ids come from. No LinkedIn account means you cannot harvest or comment later. Do not call `senders.identities.inbox_scope_get` for LinkedIn.

Preview when the owner hands you real public person-profile URLs. `tools_get` `tables.profile_watcher.preview` before `tools_run`. The fetched schema wins. Do not send a sample value from the schema. Pass at most 10 URLs. Use the owner's own public profile when they supplied it. Add a further URL only when the Ideal Customer page and `audience.local/ideal-follower.md` already name that audience. If the follower file is missing or still `Status: blank`, stop. If the Ideal Customer page is empty, stop. Do not invent a URL and do not open an interview. `tables.profile_watcher.preview` is free, returns `preview_hash`, and does not contact the network or collect. It does not schedule, it creates nothing, and it does not need a LinkedIn sender. Show the schedule, the window, the coverage limits, and the credit ceiling this preview returned. Include issues only when that payload has them. If a field is absent, leave it absent. The preview is not a list of people. Record that ceiling as `watcher_max_credits` for a single cycle. When the preview includes an estimated cost, show that number beside the ceiling. Leave month sums and predicted charges out of the note. A changed URL or a changed cap needs a fresh preview and the new hash. Before you propose another watcher, `tools_search` for a confirmed read of watchers already stored, then `tools_get`. If no read is confirmed, say that and leave creation alone.

This skill does not activate a watcher. Do not call `tables.profile_watcher.create` or `tables.profile_watcher.resume`. Create would need that `preview_hash`, `approved: true`, and the owner's yes, because later runs can spend up to `watcher_max_credits`. The create call does not write engager rows. A watcher does not score, enroll, send a file, or message anyone. Do not describe that call as a draft. A later `tables.profile_watcher.resume` needs a fresh preview and the owner's yes. A stored watcher is daily. If a later create times out, read `runs_get` before any retry, and do not open a second watcher. People on one post are `visibility.linkedin.engagement_harvest`, and this skill does not run that harvest. Do not send `dry_run` to `tables.x_post_engagers.run`.

Qualification prep uses answers already on the Ideal Customer page and in the filled follower file. Read `audience/AGENTS.md` before you describe a score. Do not copy a sample rubric, and do not treat a sample decision file as a live result. Company and person stay apart, each score from 0 to 100. Use the pass line and the exclusions already written on the Ideal Customer page. Three later actions stay uncalled here, and each carries a separate scope and a separate cost. `tables.columns.add` only stores a definition. Fit uses `binding_spec.definition_id` of `column.ai_fit_score`, one column for the company and one for the person. If those two columns are already there, a later edit reuses them. Search, then `tools_get`, before any revise, and do not invent an update id. `column.ai` stays to-check. Do not call it. A later binding uses only keys a read of that worksheet returned. `tables.columns.run` is the model run, with `max_rows` in params. A later dry run shows the prompt, the bound fields, and the price, on one row the owner can judge, then a handful: a clear fit, a wrong company, a wrong person, and a missing fact. That dry run does not judge the row. The live run is a further yes, polled with `runs_get`. `tables.table_runs.preview_cost` can price a column that is not saved yet. `tables.auto_run.set` is the third action. It spends inside `max_credits_per_run` when new rows arrive, waits for its own yes, and stays apart from the watcher. Do not call `tables.columns.add`, `tables.columns.run`, `tables.table_runs.preview_cost`, or `tables.auto_run.set` from this skill. A later rerun reads the cells already stored. A rubric change leaves old cells in place. A change in the evidence leaves the old score in place until a new run. Widen only after the owner accepts that handful. A row whose required evidence is still missing stays a hold until a new run. A blank employer, a blank role, or a missing fact stays unknown. Missing evidence stays unsure. A required fail backed by evidence on the row scores 0 on that side. A company name does not establish a domain or an identity match. Keep each person's evidence on that person. A colleague at the same company is another row. Engagement does not answer the qualifying test. Keep a reaction or a recent touch beside the decision, after fit is known. A required unknown is a hold. When the company side fails on evidence, stop, even if the person side would pass. A pass is a rubric result. Enrollment and sending wait for a separate yes. Do not call `visibility.research.web_search` or `visibility.research.urls_fetch` to fill a blank.

This skill does not post, comment, send, enroll, launch, spend, schedule, delete, react in public, or send a calendar invite. Any of those needs the owner's yes on that exact item, in a later skill. A request to connect or to preview does not turn collection on and does not start outreach. Do not draft or send here. Do not open a sequence. Do not call `sequence.sequences.activate_audience`, `sequence.sequences.resume`, or `workflow.workflows.activate`. Do not open a second database. Do not call a video-host API. Return the workspace, the three lists, the preview when you ran one, and the next step. An accepted preview quotes a configuration. Nothing in this skill was sent.
