# Draft

Make one useful point.

Name its source, the reader, and the funnel job.

Work from the brief and the files already here.

Ask only when a missing fact would change the point.

Do not invent a milestone, an anecdote, a customer quote, a revenue figure, or a line about what most people do.

Posts already in this repo are examples, not proof.

There is no separate formats file.

Let the material pick the shape, and keep it.

Steps in order when the piece is a process.

A story when a real change happened.

Evidence when the point is a claim.

A comparison when the reader is choosing.

The opening is `write-opening-lines`.

The body has to carry it.

Prefer a concrete observation they made.

Cut manufactured suspense.

Match the rhythm and the words in their own lines.

Do not add slang, self-deprecation, a deliberate mistake, a staged reversal, or a number just so the post sounds human.

Each paragraph adds information the lines above did not already give.

Remove generic praise, a superlative the source does not support, a moral said twice, a transition that carries no fact, and a slogan ending.

Keep an explanation or a qualification the reader needs.

Read the opening, the first line of each paragraph, and the ending as one argument.

Verify facts, quotes, dates, and numbers.

Each one has to match the file it came from, and that file has to be cleared for public use.

Keep a reported result, a target, and an example in separate sentences.

Use a call to action only when it matches the funnel job and a real offer exists.

Signup means the file, the keyword, and the sentence are already in `magnets/<slug>/`.

A reach close does not ask them to book.

The last line is an ask or a question.

Repurposing keeps one argument from the source.

It does not squeeze the whole source into the post.

On a revision, make the change they asked for first, and honor their wording: their facts, and the phrases that still say the thing.

Deliver one paste-ready draft.

Source notes, the uncalibrated label, and anything still unverified sit outside the copy.

The post contains no commentary on how it was written.

Offer another opening only when they ask, or when the body cannot support the line.

That opening still comes from `write-opening-lines`.
