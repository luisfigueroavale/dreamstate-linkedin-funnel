# Rows

A company claim needs that company's publication, plus a second publisher when a sentence depends on it. Write the publication date and the observation date, or write that the publication date is missing.

Prefer how the work runs, and prefer a measured result. Say what each title supports. Lead with the conclusion, the strongest evidence, and the meaning for that reader. Confidence names its limit.

Each row records the claim, the path (URL or file), an excerpt or a paraphrase, the date, the confidence, and the use. Keep evidence, inference, conflict, and open questions apart.
