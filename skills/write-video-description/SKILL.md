# Write a video description

Write a paste-ready description, links, and chapters from the finished cut or a transcript that already has times. Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when

- Use this after the cut exists, or when a transcript already carries times.

- It does not upload or invent a timestamp.

## Steps

1. Start from the finished cut, or from a transcript that already carries times, and read the promise on the brief and the offer links the owner already wrote (`references/opener.md`).

2. Write the opener as one sentence of no more than about 16 words (`references/opener.md`).

3. Include a resource when it helps this video, and a video resource uses `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=` plus the id (`references/links.md`).

4. Set `utm_source=youtube` and `utm_campaign` on the owner's own site, and a pinned-comment link carries its own `utm_content` (`references/links.md`).

5. Take chapter starts from the cut when the cut exists (`references/chapters.md`).

6. Hand over the description ready to paste, then the chapters (`references/handoff.md`). A title belongs to `write-video-titles`, and an upload belongs to `publish-youtube-video`.
## Dreamstate tools

None. Local files only.

## Stop rules

- Follow the skill-frame writing rules.

- If the only text is a script, or a transcript with no times, mark each time provisional.

- Do not treat those lines as finished chapters.

- Do not add another sentence to the opener.

- Do not pack extra search phrases into the opener.

- Do not close the opener with a hashtag block.

- Never shorten that address.

- Leave every other site free of those parameters.

- Do not make up a sponsor, a file to download, or a product the brief does not name.

- Do not invent a timestamp or a view count.

- This skill does not upload.
