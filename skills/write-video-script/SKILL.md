---
name: write-video-script
description: "Write a script the owner can record, with a viewer promise, sourced beats, and a payoff that the footage will actually show. Use before filming. It does not generate footage."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Start after `choose-next-video` passes its three gates. Keep the gate 3 opening minute unless the owner rewrites it. Deliver the words and the shot list. Do not render, publish, or generate footage.

Read the named source and the article. Walk today's real workflow. Read `brand.local/voice.md` and the named samples. The viewer is the reader in `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`. If that file is missing or `Status: blank`, stop and ask.

Say what they will know, and what they can do, when it stops. Deliver a read-aloud script, an outline, and a shot list, in that order.

1. Promise the end state first.
2. Give the context they need next.
3. Demonstrate the steps in order, with no click.
4. State what this will not do, and where it fails.
5. Show a payoff the footage can capture.
6. Name one next action to take.

`write-video-titles` writes the titles. That claim is the promise.

Each beat states the spoken copy, the visual, and the source. The transition names the next picture. A visual is a screen, a gesture, or an object in the frame. A source is a file, a page, or a walked step. No source means no beat.

Put the prerequisites before the step. Give setup friction its own beat: a login, a sample file, a permission, or a first-run dialog.

Show the real output when it is present. Name the file or the screen. Label a proposed screen, and label a placeholder. Never describe a broken feature as if it were shown. An unrun step stays unshown.

Match the requested runtime. Read the spoken lines aloud. Cut a line you cannot finish in one breath. Time it, then trim or restore. Add no lines to fill the clock. Bracket the on-screen beats.

Return the script and the shot list. Name the bracket, the visual, and the source. List a missing screen, file, number, or clip. Speak a number only when the source or the walk returned it.

`write-video-description` and the chapters wait for the final-cut transcript. `design-video-thumbnail` follows the title. Promise only what the video can show. Run `brand/taste.md` before the owner sees the pages. Nothing was recorded.
