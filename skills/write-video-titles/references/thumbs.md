# Thumbnails

Judge the thumbnail ideas at 320×180. From the shortlist, note where the words sit, whether you see a face or a screen, how large a numeral is, the colour, and which element the eye reaches first.

Write four to six concepts as text, visual, colour, and what not to repeat. Put one promise on each. Put two to five words on the image.

Return the shortlist with URLs, both titles with claim status, the concepts, and whatever the owner still has to confirm before publishing.
