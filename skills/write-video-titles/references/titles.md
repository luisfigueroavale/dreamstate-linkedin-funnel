# Titles

Name the real outcome in one line. Pick two different structures, not two phrasings of one hook. Put a risky claim, or a numeric one, in parentheses or in the second clause, so that piece can be replaced on its own. On every claim, write one label: proven, on-video-only, or hypothesis.

- Proven: the owner can show it, and any number is already on the Proof page.

- On-video-only: the footage will show it, and it is not a standing public metric.

- Hypothesis: the video does not show it yet. Packaging only.

Write Variant A, then Variant B. Give each a line on why that structure fits this video.
