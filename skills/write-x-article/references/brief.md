# Brief

Write or revise a native X article, or a long illustrated post. The announcement post for a video stays in this skill.

Adapt the argument the owner already approved. In a week, that argument is the letter. With no letter, use the transcript, framework, post, or giveaway they named. The words and the pictures describe that same argument.

Ground the brief before any sentence. Read the source itself, the Proof page, `brand.local/identity.md`, `brand.local/voice.md`, `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`, `vault/notes/pillars.md`, and `vault/notes/funnel-stages.md`. How they sound comes from `brand.local/voice.md`. This opening is not a LinkedIn hook: do not shrink it to about 210 characters, and do not force the last line to ask.

The brief names the reader's problem, the thesis, what the owner contributed, the evidence, the section order, the pillar, the funnel job, and the pictures. If a note is empty, say so. Ask only when the answer would change the argument.
