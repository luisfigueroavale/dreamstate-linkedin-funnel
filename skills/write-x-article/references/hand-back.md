# Hand back

Return the title, the article, the cover path, each inline path with its place, and any fact still open. Do not publish. Do not schedule. Do not call `social.content.schedule` or `social.content.approve`.

Do not call `social.content.article_create` (no cover field, and it cannot schedule), `social.content.article_create_schedule` (it refuses a date; `scheduled_date` omitted or null is the only form that call accepts), or `social.content.article_delivery_create` (two of its shapes publish).

Hand the draft and the picture files to the owner. They paste both into X's article editor. Tell them this desk did not post anything.

If they ask to store the video post, `tools_get` `social.content.artifact_create` and save one draft. Fetch the shape of the channel field. x is one of the writable channels the contract named. Re-fetch, because that list moves. That save does not publish. Do not put the long article in that post artifact.

Before you hand it back, check five things. The title promises a useful outcome. The opening holds the strongest result the Proof page supports. The owner would recognize the reasoning. A reader can scan the piece and act. The words, the pictures, and the links describe one system.
