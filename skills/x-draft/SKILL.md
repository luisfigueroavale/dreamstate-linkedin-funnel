description: "Draft a short X post or thread from a sourced idea in the owner's voice. Use for X copy. It does not publish."
---

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

Read the brief, `vault/notes/`, and the post file. The reader is `audience.local/ideal-follower.md`. Proof is only what `brand.local/identity.md` clears. Match their vocabulary and their rhythm from `brand.local/voice.md` and the samples in `vault/voice/`. A missing or blank voice file still allows a neutral draft, and you say the draft does not match their voice. A blank identity file still stops you.

Keep it short. One post when one post can finish the idea. A thread only when the explanation needs several distinct steps. Number the posts in order. The opening is a specific point the later lines can support. Each post adds information the reader did not already have, and it connects to the post before it. No fake cliffhanger. No repeated conclusion.

Do not chop a LinkedIn draft into a thread. If the claim needs the letter, link the letter.

Do not invent slang, outrage, controversy, an anecdote, or a screenshot to imitate the platform. No invented metric.

The link and the attribution sit beside the claim they support. The owner's site, and the letter, use `utm_source=x` and the weekly campaign slug on the brief. Do not invent that slug. A video URL stays the full https URL. Do not shorten it.

When they care about an exact fit, check the current limit for that account and that format, including how a link is counted. A plain character count is only an approximation of composer weighting. Use the composer, or a counter that applies the same weighting, when you have one.

Deliver paste-ready copy. Source notes stay outside that copy. A picture, if they asked, is `frame-build`. Store the copy only if they ask: `tools_get`, then `social.content.artifact_create`. Drafting does not publish and does not start reply automation. Do not schedule, do not approve, and do not call `sequence.x.auto_engagements.replace`.
