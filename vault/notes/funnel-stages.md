# Funnel jobs

`pillar-map` uses these three jobs. A pillar gets one. The names below are the definitions. The owner's pillars live in `vault/notes/pillars.md`.

Status: blank

## Reach

The file has to be seen. The piece is the recording, the native upload, or a post whose only job is attention. The close does not ask for a booking.

## Belief

The argument has to change a mind. The letter is written first. The X essay, the lesson, and the posts that teach reuse that argument. They do not add a new proof.

## Signup

The resource post asks the reader to take the file. The keyword, the full URL, and the sentence they will receive are already in `magnets/<slug>/`. This job is the ask. It is not a pitch sequence.
